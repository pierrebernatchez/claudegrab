Exponential and Logarithmic Formulas
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :width: 100%
   :header-rows: 1

   * - :math:`A(t) = A_0(1+i)^t`
     - :math:`A(t) = A_0\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{t/H}`
     - :math:`A(t) = A_0(2)^{t/D}`
   * - where :math:`i` is percent growth (+) or decay (-)
     - where :math:`H` is the half-life period
     - where :math:`D` is the doubling period
   * - :math:`pH = -\log[H^+]`
     - :math:`\beta_2 - \beta_1 = 10\log\left(\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}\right)`
     - :math:`M = \log\left(\dfrac{I}{I_0}\right)`
   * - where :math:`pH` is acidity and :math:`[H^+]` is concentration
       of hydronium ions in mol/L
     - where :math:`\beta` is the loudness in dB and :math:`I` is the
       intensity of sound in :math:`\text{W/m}^2`
     - where :math:`M` is the magnitude measured by richters and
       :math:`I` is intensity
