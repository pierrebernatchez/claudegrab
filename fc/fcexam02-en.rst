Formula Page

TRIG IDENTITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Sine Law:** :math:`\dfrac{a}{\sin A} = \dfrac{b}{\sin B} = \dfrac{c}{\sin C}`
**Cosine Law:** :math:`c^2 = a^2+b^2-2ab\cos C`

**Reciprocal Functions:** :math:`\csc x = \dfrac{1}{\sin x}`;
:math:`\sec x = \dfrac{1}{\cos x}`; :math:`\cot x = \dfrac{1}{\tan x}`

**Quotient Identities:** :math:`\tan x = \dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}`;
:math:`\cot x = \dfrac{\cos x}{\sin x}`

**Even/Odd Functions:** :math:`\cos(-x)=\cos x`; :math:`\sin(-x)=-\sin x`

**Pythagorean Identities:** :math:`\sin^2 x+\cos^2 x=1`;
:math:`\cos^2 x=1-\sin^2 x`; :math:`\sin^2 x=1-\cos^2 x`

:math:`\tan^2 x+1=\sec^2 x`; :math:`1+\cot^2 x=\csc^2 x`

**Transformation Identities:** :math:`\cos\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)=\sin x`;
:math:`\sin\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)=\cos x`

**Co-function Identities:** :math:`\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x\right)=\sin x`;
:math:`\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x\right)=\cos x`

**Compound Angle Formulas:**

:math:`\sin(x+y)=\sin x\cos y+\cos x\sin y`;
:math:`\sin(x-y)=\sin x\cos y-\cos x\sin y`

:math:`\cos(x+y)=\cos x\cos y-\sin x\sin y`;
:math:`\cos(x-y)=\cos x\cos y+\sin x\sin y`

:math:`\tan(x+y)=\dfrac{\tan x+\tan y}{1-\tan x\tan y}`;
:math:`\tan(x-y)=\dfrac{\tan x-\tan y}{1+\tan x\tan y}`

**Double Angle Formulas:**

:math:`\sin(2x)=2\sin x\cos x`

:math:`\cos(2x)=\cos^2 x-\sin^2 x`; :math:`\cos(2x)=2\cos^2 x-1`;
:math:`\cos(2x)=1-2\sin^2 x`

:math:`\tan(2x)=\dfrac{2\tan x}{1-\tan^2 x}`

OTHER FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Quadratic Formula:** :math:`x = \dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}`
**Newton Quotient:** :math:`f'(x) = \displaystyle\lim_{h\to 0}
\dfrac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}`

**Difference of Squares:** :math:`a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)`
**Perfect Square Trinomial:** :math:`a^2+2ab+b^2=(a+b)^2`

**Sum of Cubes:** :math:`a^3+b^3=(a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2)`
**Difference of Cubes:** :math:`a^3-b^3=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)`

   * - :math:`A(t) = A_0(1+i)^t`
     - :math:`A(t) = A_0\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{t/H}`
     - :math:`A(t) = A_0(2)^{t/D}`
   * - where :math:`i` is percent growth (+) or decay (-)
