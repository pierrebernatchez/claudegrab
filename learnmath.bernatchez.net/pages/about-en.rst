**Description**

The goal of this site is to share teaching materials among teachers and students.

The site is organized like a blog.

Recent contributions will appear in the main page.  

**Structure**

You have 4 ways of finding articles.

1. The home page lists the most recent articles chronologically.
2. Categories groups articles by category.
3. Keywords groups articles by keyword.
4. Archives list a table of all articles chronologically.
