**9)** A circle of radius 25 cm has a central angle of 4.75 radians.
Determine the length of the arc that subtends this angle.

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \theta &=& \dfrac{a}{r} \\
   4.75 &=& \dfrac{a}{25} \\
   a &=& 118.75 \text{ cm}
   \end{array}

**10)** Two highways meet at an angle measuring :math:`\dfrac{\pi}{3}`
radians, as shown. An on-ramp in the shape of a circular arc is to be
built such that the arc has a radius of 80 m.

.. image:: ../images/u4worksheet01-gpimage01.png
   :scale: 60
   :alt: two roads meeting at angle pi/3, with an 80 m radius on-ramp arc rounding the corner

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Determine an EXACT expression for the length of the on-ramp.

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \theta &=& \dfrac{a}{r} \\
   \dfrac{\pi}{3} &=& \dfrac{a}{80} \\
   a &=& \dfrac{80\pi}{3} \text{ m}
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) Determine the length of the on-ramp, to the nearest tenth of a meter.

:solmath:`\cong 83.8 \text{ m}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**11)** David made a swing for his niece Sarah using ropes 2.4 m long, so
that Sarah swings through an arc length of 1.2 meters. Determine the angle
through which Sarah swings, in both radians and degrees.

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \theta &=& \dfrac{a}{r} \\
   \theta &=& \dfrac{1.2}{2.4} \\
   \theta &=& 0.5 \text{ radians}
   \end{array}

:solmath:`0.5 \times \dfrac{180}{\pi} \cong 28.6°`
