Answer Key
================================================================================

**1)** a) :math:`h(t) = 10\cos\left[\dfrac{\pi}{30}(t-30)\right]+11` b)
6 m @ 10 s, 16 m @ 160 s

**2)** a) 20 minutes. Every 20 minutes the furnace completes a cycle of
heating and cooling. b) Max = 200°C, Min = 120°C, reached at :math:`x =
10` min c) 147.6°C @ 4 min, 192.4°C @ 12 min

**3)** a) 8 m b) 14 m c) high tide at 3:00 am and pm, low tide at 9:00
am and pm d) :math:`y = 6\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}x\right)+8`

**4)** a) 6 seconds per cycle b) :math:`h(t) =
12\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}x\right)+16` c) 10 cm @ 10 s, 10 cm @ 50 s

**5)** b) Vertical reflection, vertical stretch by a factor of 25,
horizontal compression by a factor of :math:`\dfrac{3}{2\pi}`, vertical
translation 27 units up c) :math:`y = -25\cos\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}x\right)+27`

**6)** a) 1.2 seconds; the time between beats of a person's heart b) 50
