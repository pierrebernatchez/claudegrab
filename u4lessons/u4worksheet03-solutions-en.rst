**4)** A boat is in the water 150 meters from a straight shoreline. There
is a rotating beam on the boat.

.. image:: ../images/u4worksheet03-gpimage03.png
   :scale: 65
   :alt: boat 150 m from shore, rotating beam at angle x hitting the shore at distance d

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Determine a reciprocal trigonometric relation for the distance,
:math:`d`, from the boat to where the light hits the shoreline in terms
of the angle of rotation :math:`x`.

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \cos x &=& \dfrac{150}{d} \\
   \sec x &=& \dfrac{d}{150}
   \end{array}

:solmath:`d = 150\sec x`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) Determine an exact expression for the distance when :math:`x =
\dfrac{\pi}{6}`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   d &=& 150\sec\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right) \\
   d &=& \dfrac{150}{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{6}\right)} \\
   d &=& \dfrac{150}{\left(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)} \\
   d &=& \dfrac{300}{\sqrt3} \text{ m}
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) Determine an approximate value, to the nearest tenth of a meter, for
the distance.

:solmath:`d \cong 173.2 \text{ m}`

**5)** A variant on the carousel at a theme park is the swing ride.
Swings are suspended from a rotating platform and move outward to form
an angle :math:`x` with the vertical as the ride rotates. The angle is
related to the radial distance, :math:`r`, in meters, from the center of
rotation; the acceleration, :math:`g = 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2`, due to
gravity; and the speed, :math:`v`, in meters per second, of the swing,
according to the formula

:math:`\cot x = \dfrac{rg}{v^2}`

Determine the angle :math:`x` for a swing located 3.5 meters from the
center of rotation and moving at 5.4 m/s, to the nearest hundredth of a
radian.

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \cot x &=& \dfrac{3.5(9.8)}{(5.4)^2} \\
   \tan x &=& \dfrac{(5.4)^2}{3.5(9.8)} \\
   \tan x &\cong& 0.850146 \\
   x &=& \tan^{-1}(0.850146) \\
   x &\cong& 0.70
   \end{array}

**6)** Explain the difference between :math:`\csc \dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}` and
:math:`\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\right)`

:sol:`The cosecant function is the reciprocal of the sine function. For`
:math:`\sin^{-1}`, :sol:`the -1 is NOT an exponent but instead a notation
meaning the opposite operation of sine. The sine function takes an angle
for an input and gives a ratio as an output.` :math:`\sin^{-1}` :sol:`takes
a ratio for an input and gives the angle as an output.`
