c\) :math:`\dfrac{3\pi}{2}`

.. image:: ../images/u4worksheet02-gpimage06.png

   :alt: CAST diagram showing terminal arm at 3 pi over 2, straight down on the y-axis

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \sin\theta &=& \dfrac{y}{r} = \dfrac{-1}{1} = -1 \\
   \cos\theta &=& \dfrac{x}{r} = \dfrac{0}{1} = 0 \\
   \tan\theta &=& \dfrac{y}{x} = \dfrac{-1}{0} = \text{undefined}
   \end{array}

**7)** Lynda is flying her kite at the end of a 40-m string. The string
makes an angle of :math:`\dfrac{\pi}{4}` with the ground. The wind speed
increases, and the kite flies higher until the string makes an angle of
:math:`\dfrac{\pi}{3}` with the ground.

.. image:: ../images/u4worksheet02-gpimage08.png
   :scale: 65
   :alt: two kite string positions from the origin at angles pi/4 and pi/3, each 40 m

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Determine an exact expression for the horizontal distance that the
kite moves between the two positions.

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \cos \dfrac{\pi}{4} &=& \dfrac{x}{40} \\
   \dfrac{1}{\sqrt2} &=& \dfrac{x}{40} \\
   x &=& \dfrac{40}{\sqrt2} \\
   x &=& 20\sqrt2
   \end{array}

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \cos \dfrac{\pi}{3} &=& \dfrac{y}{40} \\
   \dfrac{1}{2} &=& \dfrac{y}{40} \\
   y &=& 20
   \end{array}

:sol:`Horizontal distance =` :solmath:`x - y = 20\sqrt2 - 20 = 20(\sqrt2 -
1) \text{ m}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) Determine an exact expression for the vertical distance that the kite
moves between the two positions.

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \sin \dfrac{\pi}{4} &=& \dfrac{a}{40} \\
   \dfrac{1}{\sqrt2} &=& \dfrac{a}{40} \\
   a &=& \dfrac{40}{\sqrt2} \\
   a &=& 20\sqrt2
   \end{array}

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \sin \dfrac{\pi}{3} &=& \dfrac{b}{40} \\
   \dfrac{\sqrt3}{2} &=& \dfrac{b}{40} \\
   b &=& 20\sqrt3
   \end{array}

:sol:`Vertical distance =` :solmath:`b - a = 20\sqrt3 - 20\sqrt2 =
20(\sqrt3 - \sqrt2) \text{ m}`

**8)** Determine an exact value for each expression

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`\dfrac{\sin\frac{\pi}{3}\tan\frac{\pi}{6}}{\cos\frac{\pi}{4}}`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \dfrac{\sin\frac{\pi}{3}\tan\frac{\pi}{6}}{\cos\frac{\pi}{4}}
   &=& \dfrac{\left(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt3}\right)}{\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\right)} \\
   &=& \dfrac{\left(\frac12\right)}{\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\right)} \\
   &=& \dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`\cot \dfrac{5\pi}{4} + \tan \dfrac{11\pi}{6}\tan \dfrac{5\pi}{3}`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \cot \dfrac{5\pi}{4} + \tan \dfrac{11\pi}{6}\tan \dfrac{5\pi}{3}
   &=& \cot\dfrac{\pi}{4} - \tan\dfrac{\pi}{6}\left(-\tan\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) \\
   &=& 1 - \left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\right)\left(-\sqrt3\right) \\
   &=& 1 - (-1) \\
   &=& 2
   \end{array}
