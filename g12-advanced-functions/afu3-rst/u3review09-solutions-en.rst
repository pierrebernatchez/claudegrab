**20)** Solve exactly. Then use your calculator to evaluate correct to

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`3^{x-2} = 5^x`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log(3^{x-2}) &=& \log(5^x) \\
   (x-2)\log 3 &=& x\log 5 \\
   x\log 3 - 2\log 3 &=& x\log 5 \\
   x\log 3 - x\log 5 &=& 2\log 3 \\
   x(\log 3 - \log 5) &=& 2\log 3 \\
   x &=& \dfrac{2\log 3}{\log 3 - \log 5} \\
   x &\cong& -4.301
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`2^{k-2} = 3^{k+1}`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log(2^{k-2}) &=& \log(3^{k+1}) \\
   (k-2)\log 2 &=& (k+1)\log 3 \\
   k\log 2 - 2\log 2 &=& k\log 3 + \log 3 \\
   k\log 2 - k\log 3 &=& \log 3 + 2\log 2 \\
   k(\log 2 - \log 3) &=& \log 3 + \log 4 \\
   k &=& \dfrac{\log 3 + \log 4}{\log 2 - \log 3} \\
   k &\cong& -6.129
   \end{array}

Case 1

Case 2

:solmath:`4^x = -4`

:sol:`Extraneous root (no solution) -- the base of a positive power can
never equal a negative number.`

:sol:`Therefore` :solmath:`x \cong 1.16`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`2^x + 12(2)^{-x} = 7`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   (2^x)(2^x) + 12(2^x)(2^{-x}) &=& 7(2^x) \\
   2^{2x} + 12 &=& 7(2^x) \\
   (2^x)^2 - 7(2^x) + 12 &=& 0
   \end{array}

Let :math:`k = 2^x`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

**26)** When you drink a cup of coffee or a glass of cola, or when you
eat a chocolate bar, the percent, :math:`P`, of caffeine remaining in
your bloodstream is related to the elapsed time, :math:`t`, in hours by
:math:`t = 5\left(\dfrac{\log P}{\log 0.5}\right)`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) How long will it take for the amount of caffeine to drop to 20% of
the amount consumed?

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   t &=& 5\left(\dfrac{\log 0.2}{\log 0.5}\right) \\
   t &\cong& 11.61 \text{ hours}
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) Suppose you drink a cup of coffee at 9:00 am, what percent of the
caffeine will remain in your body at noon?

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   3 &=& 5\left(\dfrac{\log P}{\log 0.5}\right) \\
   \dfrac{3\log 0.5}{5} &=& \log P \\
   P &=& 10^{\frac{3\log 0.5}{5}} \\
   P &\cong& 0.6598
   \end{array}

:sol:`About 66% remains.`

**27)** A 50-mg sample of cobalt-60 decays to 40 mg after 1.6 minutes.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Determine the half-life of cobalt-60.

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   40 &=& 50\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{1.6}{h}} \\
   0.8 &=& 0.5^{\frac{1.6}{h}} \\
   \log 0.8 &=& \dfrac{1.6}{h}\log 0.5 \\
   h &=& \dfrac{1.6\log 0.5}{\log 0.8} \\
   h &\cong& 5 \text{ minutes}
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How long will it take for the sample to decay to 5% of its initial
amount?

.. math::

   2.5 &=& 50\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{t}{5}} \\
   0.05 &=& \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{t}{5}} \\
   \log 0.05 &=& \dfrac{t}{5}\log\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right) \\
   t &=& \dfrac{5\log 0.05}{\log 0.5} \\
   t &\cong& 21.61 \text{ minutes}
   \end{array}

**35)** An altimeter is a device that measures the height of a plane
above the ground. It works based on air pressure according to the
formula :math:`h = 18\,400\log\dfrac{P_0}{P}`, where :math:`h` is the
height above the ground in metres, :math:`P` is the air pressure at
that height, and :math:`P_0` was the air pressure on the ground at
takeoff. Air pressure is measured in kilopascals (kPa).

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Air pressure on the ground was 102 kPa. If the airplane instruments
measure a pressure of 32.5 kPa outside the plane, what is the height of
the airplane to the nearest metre?

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   h &=& 18\,400\log\left(\dfrac{102}{32.5}\right) \\
   h &\cong& 9140 \text{ m}
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) What is the outside air pressure for a plane flying at 11 000
metres? Assume a ground pressure of 102.5 kPa. Round to one decimal
place.

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   11\,000 &=& 18\,400\log\left(\dfrac{102.5}{P}\right) \\
   \dfrac{11\,000}{18\,400} &=& \log\left(\dfrac{102.5}{P}\right) \\
   10^{\frac{11\,000}{18\,400}} &=& \dfrac{102.5}{P} \\
   P &=& \dfrac{102.5}{10^{\frac{11\,000}{18\,400}}} \\
   P &\cong& 25.9 \text{ kPa}
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) How high would a plane have to be flying when it encountered air
pressure in the air that was half the air pressure on the ground? Round
to the nearest metre.

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   h &=& 18\,400\log(2) \\
   h &\cong& 5539 \text{ m}
   \end{array}
