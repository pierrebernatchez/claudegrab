**3)** An influenza virus is spreading according to the function
:math:`N = 10(2)^t`, where :math:`N` is the number of people infected and
:math:`t` is the time, in days.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) How many people have the virus at each time?

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

i\) initially, when :math:`t = 0`

:solmath:`N = 10`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

ii\) after 1 day

:solmath:`N = 10(2)^1 = 20`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

iii\) after 2 days

:solmath:`N = 10(2)^2 = 40`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

iv\) after 3 days

:solmath:`N = 10(2)^3 = 80`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) After how many days will 40 960 people be infected?

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   40\,960 &=& 10(2)^t \\
   4096 &=& 2^t \\
   \log 4096 &=& \log 2^t \\
   \log 4096 &=& t \log 2 \\
   t &=& \dfrac{\log 4096}{\log 2} \\
   t &=& 12 \text{ days}
   \end{array}
