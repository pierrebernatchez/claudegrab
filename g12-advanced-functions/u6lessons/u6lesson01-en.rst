Part 1: Terminology
================================================================================

``____________________``: a measure of the change in one quantity (the
dependent variable) with respect to a change in another quantity (the
independent variable).

``____________________``: a line that passes through two points on the
graph of a relation

``____________________``: a line that touches the graph of a relation at
only one point within a small interval
