Unit 1 Revision Plan — Summary of Feedback from Our Meeting (2026-09-12)
================================================================================

Thank you again for taking the time to review Unit 1 with me. Below is a
recap of everything you flagged, organized by topic, along with the plan
for addressing each item.

WORDING AND TERMINOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. "Power function(s)" will be corrected to "polynomial power
   function(s)" everywhere it appears (Lesson 1, Worksheet 1, and the
   later lessons that reference it), in both English and French.
2. "n is a whole number" will be changed to "n is a natural number" in
   Lesson 1's Polynomial Functions section (two places).
3. Lesson titles will include a unit/lesson number, e.g. "1.1", so you
   can easily direct students to the right section.

FIGURES AND DIAGRAMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. The number line diagram in Lesson 1 will get a proper line with
   arrowheads on both ends (currently just tick marks with no
   connecting line or arrows).
5. The two symmetry diagrams (line symmetry and point symmetry) will
   have their stray, purposeless arrows removed.
6. The point symmetry diagram's "(a, b)" label will get an actual point
   marker to label, since currently the label has nothing to point to.
7. We will double-check that every plotted graph in Unit 1 clearly
   shows the x and y axes.
8. In Lesson 2, one graph (the quartic with two low points) will be
   adjusted so it has one clear absolute minimum instead of two
   mathematically tied ones, matching what the text says.
9. In Lesson 2, another graph's vertical scale will be adjusted so all
   of its x-intercepts are actually visible (currently one crossing is
   too shallow to see at the chosen scale).
10. We will review all of Unit 1's graphs to make sure the scale is
    appropriately zoomed in wherever a graph currently looks
    unnecessarily large, especially in Lesson 2.
11. Any English text baked directly into a figure (as opposed to the
    surrounding paragraph) will be removed, since those figures are
    shared between the English and French versions and currently leak
    English words into the French document in at least one spot.

LAYOUT AND READABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. In Lesson 2, a phrase that reads "On the graph of this function..."
    will be reworded to explicitly say "the graph below," and that text
    will be kept on the same page as the graph it describes (right now
    a page break can separate them, which is confusing).
13. A similar page-break fix will be applied to the "odd degree
    polynomial extends from..." explanation and the pair of graphs
    below it in Lesson 2.
14. Lesson 2's Example 1 (parts a and b) will be reformatted out of its
    current boxed/table layout into our standard format, matching the
    rest of the document.
15. Fill-in-the-blank questions will get more vertical writing space —
    a general pass, low priority, only if straightforward.
16. Blank table cells (where students write an answer inside a table)
    will be sized to at least 32pt tall so there's enough room to write.

PAGE NUMBERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
17. Page numbers will be added to every document going forward, so you
    can tell students exactly which page to look at. This is a
    rendering change that applies automatically once made, so it will
    also appear on already-existing documents once they're re-generated.

ADDITIONAL ITEMS RAISED (apply beyond just Unit 1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
18. Worksheets should include the short answer key from the original
    source material (in addition to the full worked-solutions document
    we already produce), so students can check their own work. This
    applies to every worksheet built so far, not just Unit 1's.
19. In the solutions documents, the parts that were added to show the
    worked answer (as opposed to the original question) will be shown
    in red, matching the style of the original source material.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A few of these items have an open question attached (e.g. exact title
numbering format, whether certain fixes extend to a second, similar
spot) that we'll resolve internally before finalizing — nothing that
should require another round with you unless something comes up.
