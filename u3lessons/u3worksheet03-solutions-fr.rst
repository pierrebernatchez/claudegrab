**1)** Simplifie à l'aide des lois des logarithmes, puis évalue.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`\log 9 + \log 6`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log 9 + \log 6 &=& \log(9 \times 6) \\
   &=& \log(54) \\
   &=& 1.732
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`\log 48 - \log 6`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log 48 - \log 6 &=& \log\left(\dfrac{48}{6}\right) \\
   &=& \log(8) \\
   &=& 0.903
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) :math:`\log_3 7 + \log_3 3`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log_3 7 + \log_3 3 &=& \log_3(7 \times 3) \\
   &=& \log_3(21) \\
   &=& \dfrac{\log 21}{\log 3} \\
   &=& 2.771
   \end{array}

:solmath:`= \log(2xyz)`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`\log_2 a + \log_2(3b) - \log_2(2c)`

:solmath:`= \log_2\left(\dfrac{3ab}{2c}\right)`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) :math:`2 \log m + 3 \log n - 4 \log y`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   2 \log m + 3 \log n - 4 \log y &=& \log m^2 + \log n^3 - \log y^4 \\
   &=& \log\left(\dfrac{m^2 n^3}{y^4}\right)
   \end{array}

**5)** Évalue, à l'aide des lois des logarithmes

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`3 \log_{16} 2 + 2 \log_{16} 8 - \log_{16} 2`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   3 \log_{16} 2 + 2 \log_{16} 8 - \log_{16} 2 &=& \log_{16}(2^3) + \log_{16}(8^2) - \log_{16}(2) \\
   &=& \log_{16}(8) + \log_{16}(64) - \log_{16}(2) \\
   &=& \log_{16}(256) \\
   &=& 2
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`\log 20 + \log 2 + \dfrac{1}{3} \log 125`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log 20 + \log 2 + \dfrac{1}{3} \log 125 &=& \log(20 \times 2) + \log\left(125^{\frac13}\right) \\
   &=& \log(40) + \log(5) \\
   &=& \log(40 \times 5) \\
   &=& \log(200) \\
   &\cong& 2.301
   \end{array}

**6)** Écris comme une somme ou une différence de logarithmes.
Simplifie, si possible.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`\log_7(cd)`

:solmath:`= \log_7 c + \log_7 d`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`\log_3\left(\dfrac{m}{n}\right)`

:solmath:`= \log_3 m - \log_3 n`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) :math:`\log(uv^3)`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log(uv^3) &=& \log u + \log v^3 \\
   &=& \log u + 3 \log v
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

d\) :math:`\log\left(\dfrac{a\sqrt{b}}{c^2}\right)`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log\left(\dfrac{a\sqrt{b}}{c^2}\right) &=& \log a + \log b^{\frac12} - \log c^2 \\
   &=& \log a + \dfrac{1}{2}\log b - 2\log c
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

e\) :math:`\log_2 10`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log_2 10 &=& \log_2(2 \times 5) \\
   &=& \log_2 2 + \log_2 5 \\
   &=& 1 + \log_2 5
   \end{array}

**7)** Simplifie

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`\log\left(\dfrac{x^2}{\sqrt{x}}\right)`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log\left(\dfrac{x^2}{\sqrt{x}}\right) &=& \log x^2 - \log x^{\frac12} \\
   &=& 2\log x - \dfrac{1}{2}\log x \\
   &=& \dfrac{3}{2}\log x
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`\log\sqrt{k} + \log(\sqrt{k})^3 + \log\sqrt[3]{k^2}`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log\sqrt{k} + \log(\sqrt{k})^3 + \log\sqrt[3]{k^2} &=& \log k^{\frac12} + \log k^{\frac32} + \log k^{\frac23} \\
   &=& \dfrac{1}{2}\log k + \dfrac{3}{2}\log k + \dfrac{2}{3}\log k \\
   &=& \dfrac{3}{6}\log k + \dfrac{9}{6}\log k + \dfrac{4}{6}\log k \\
   &=& \dfrac{16}{6}\log k \\
   &=& \dfrac{8}{3}\log k
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) :math:`\log(x^2 - 4) - \log(x - 2)`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log(x^2 - 4) - \log(x - 2) &=& \log\left(\dfrac{x^2 - 4}{x - 2}\right) \\
   &=& \log\left[\dfrac{(x-2)(x+2)}{x-2}\right] \\
   &=& \log(x + 2)
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

d\) :math:`\log(x^2 - x - 6) - \log(2x - 6)`

.. math::
   :nowrap:
   :class: solution

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log(x^2 - x - 6) - \log(2x - 6) &=& \log\left(\dfrac{x^2 - x - 6}{2x - 6}\right) \\
   &=& \log\left[\dfrac{(x-3)(x+2)}{2(x-3)}\right] \\
   &=& \log\left(\dfrac{x+2}{2}\right)
   \end{array}
