**1)** Evaluate.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`\log_2 16^3`

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log_2 16^3 &=& 3 \log_2(2^4) \\
   &=& 3(4) \\
   &=& 12
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`\log_4 8^2`

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log_4 8^2 &=& \log_4 64 \\
   &=& \log_4 (4^3) \\
   &=& 3
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) :math:`\log 100^{-4}`

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log 100^{-4} &=& -4 \log(10^2) \\
   &=& -4(2) \\
   &=& -8
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

d\) :math:`\log 0.1^{\frac{1}{2}}`

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log 0.1^{\frac{1}{2}} &=& \dfrac{1}{2} \log(10^{-1}) \\
   &=& \dfrac{1}{2}(-1) \\
   &=& -\dfrac{1}{2}
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

e\) :math:`\log_2 \sqrt{8}`

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log_2 \sqrt{8} &=& \dfrac{1}{2} \log_2(2^3) \\
   &=& \dfrac{1}{2}(3) \\
   &=& \dfrac{3}{2}
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

f\) :math:`\log_3 \left(\sqrt[3]{81}\right)^6`

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log_3 \left(\sqrt[3]{81}\right)^6 &=& \log_3 (81)^2 \\
   &=& 2 \log_3(3^4) \\
   &=& 2(4) \\
   &=& 8
   \end{array}

**3)** An investment earns 7% interest, compounded annually. The amount,
:math:`A`, that the investment is worth as a function of time, :math:`t`,
in years, is given by :math:`A(t) = 500(1.07)^t`.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Use the equation to determine the value of the investment after 4
years.

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   A(4) &=& 500(1.07)^4 \\
   &=& \$655.40
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How long will it take for the investment to double in value?

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   1000 &=& 500(1.07)^t \\
   2 &=& 1.07^t \\
   \log_{1.07} 2 &=& t \\
   \dfrac{\log 2}{\log 1.07} &=& t \\
   t &=& 10.2 \text{ years}
   \end{array}

**6)a)** Evaluate :math:`\log_2 8^5` without using the power law of
logarithms.

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log_2 8^5 &=& \log_2 (2^3)^5 \\
   &=& \log_2 (2^{15}) \\
   &=& 15
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**b)** Evaluate the same expression by applying the power law of
logarithms.

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   \log_2 8^5 &=& 5 \log_2(2^3) \\
   &=& 5(3) \\
   &=& 15
   \end{array}

**7)** Solve for :math:`x`, correct to 3 decimal places.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`2 = \log 3^x`

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   2 &=& x \log 3 \\
   x &=& \dfrac{2}{\log 3} \\
   x &=& 4.192
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`100 = 10 \log 1000^x`

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   10 &=& \log 1000^x \\
   10 &=& x \log 1000 \\
   x &=& \dfrac{10}{\log 1000} \\
   x &=& 3.333
   \end{array}

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) :math:`4 = \log_3 15^x`

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{array}{rcl}
   3^4 &=& 15^x \\
   \log 81 &=& x \log 15 \\
   x &=& \dfrac{\log 81}{\log 15} \\
   x &=& 1.623
   \end{array}
