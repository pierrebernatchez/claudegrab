Answer Key
================================================================================

**1)** a) :math:`19` b) :math:`15` c) :math:`13` d) :math:`12` e) :math:`11.4`
f) :math:`11.04`

**2)** a) :math:`15` m/s b) :math:`-5` m/s

**3)** :math:`4`; :math:`4`; the average rate of change is always 4
because the function is linear, with a slope of 4.

**4)** a) i) :math:`750` ppl/year ii) :math:`3000` ppl/year
iii) :math:`12\,000` ppl/year iv) :math:`5250` ppl/year
b) no, the rate of growth increases as the time increases.

**5)** a) i) :math:`\$2.60`/sweatshirt ii) :math:`\$2.00`/sweatshirt
iii) :math:`\$1.40`/sweatshirt iv) :math:`\$0.80`/sweatshirt
b) The rate of change is still positive, but it is decreasing. This means
that the profit is still increasing, but at a decreasing rate.
c) No; after 6000 sweatshirts are sold, the rate of change becomes
negative. This means that the profit begins to decrease after 6000
sweatshirts are sold.
