**1\)** Identify which of the following are polynomial functions:

a\) :math:`p(x) = \cos x` --- :sol:`No`

b\) :math:`h(x) = -7x` --- :sol:`Yes`

c\) :math:`f(x) = 2x^4` --- :sol:`Yes`

d\) :math:`y = 3x^5 - 2x^3 + x^2 - 1` --- :sol:`Yes`

e\) :math:`k(x) = 8^x` --- :sol:`No`

f\) :math:`y = x^{-3}` --- :sol:`No`
