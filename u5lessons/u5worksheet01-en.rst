Answer Key
================================================================================

**1)** a) :math:`\tan x` b) :math:`1` c) :math:`\sin^2 x` d) :math:`\cos^2 x`
e) :math:`\sec x` f) :math:`\cos^2 x` g) :math:`\cos x` h) :math:`\csc^2 x`
i) :math:`\tan x` j) :math:`\tan x`

**3)** Answers will vary depending but possible solutions are:

a) :math:`y = \cos(\theta+2\pi)`, :math:`y = \cos(\theta-2\pi)`,
:math:`y = \cos(\theta-4\pi)`

b) :math:`y = \sin\left(\theta+\dfrac{5\pi}{2}\right)`,
:math:`y = \sin\left(\theta-\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\right)`,
:math:`y = \sin\left(\theta-\dfrac{7\pi}{2}\right)`

**4)** a) :math:`\cos\dfrac{\pi}{3}` b) :math:`\sin\dfrac{\pi}{12}`
c) :math:`\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{16}`

**5)** a) :math:`\sin\dfrac{\pi}{8}` b) :math:`-\cos\dfrac{\pi}{12}`
c) :math:`\cos\dfrac{\pi}{6}`

**9)** a) :math:`\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{22} = \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{11} \approx 0.6549`
b) :math:`\sin\dfrac{17\pi}{22} = \cos\left(-\dfrac{3\pi}{11}\right) = \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{11} \approx 0.6549`
