Answer Key
================================================================================

**1.** a) Yes b) No

**2.** a) :math:`(6,0,0)`, :math:`(0,-4,0)`, :math:`(0,0,2)` b)
:math:`(4,0,0)`, no :math:`y`-int, :math:`(0,0,-2)`

**3.** a) :math:`\pi: \begin{cases} x = 3+4t+5s \\ y = -1-2t+s \\ z =
2+3t \end{cases}` b) :math:`\pi: \begin{cases} x = t+s \\ y = 8-4s \\
z = 7-3t+7s \end{cases}`

**4.** a) :math:`[x,y,z] = [1,8,-1] + t[-9,-7,-3] + s[4,1,2]` b)
:math:`[x,y,z] = [4,3,0] + t[-1,0,2] + s[-1,4,0]`

**5.** :math:`x`-int: :math:`\left(-\dfrac{6}{7},0,0\right)`,
:math:`y`-int: :math:`(0,-6,0)`, :math:`z`-int:
:math:`\left(0,0,\dfrac{6}{5}\right)`

**6.** a) :math:`[x,y,z] = [0,0,0] + t[2,-1,7] + s[3,5,2]` b)
:math:`[x,y,z] = [1,-2,3] + t[4,1,5] + s[2,11,-1]`

**7.** :math:`[x,y,z] = [2,-1,4] + t[-5,5,1] + s[6,0,2]`
