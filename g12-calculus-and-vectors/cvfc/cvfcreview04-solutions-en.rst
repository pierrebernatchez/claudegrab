**6)** The diagram shows a parallelepiped. Determine a single vector that is equivalent to each sum or

   :widths: 50 50

   :width: 100%

   * - **a)** :math:`\vec{AB} - \vec{BF}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\vec{AB} - \vec{BF} = \vec{EB}`
     - **b)** :math:`\vec{AB} + \vec{CG}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

   * - **c)** :math:`\vec{EF} + \vec{DH}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :solmath:`\vec{EF} + \vec{DH} = \vec{AB} + \vec{BF}`

**8)** Given that :math:`|\vec{u}| = 12` and :math:`|\vec{v}| = 5` and the angle between :math:`\vec{u}` and
:math:`\vec{v}` is :math:`30°`, determine:

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - **a)** the unit vector in the direction of :math:`\vec{u} + \vec{v}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       The angle between :math:`\vec{u}` and :math:`\vec{v}` placed tail to tail is :math:`30°`, so the
       interior angle of the triangle formed is :math:`150°`:

       :solmath:`|\vec{u}+\vec{v}|^2 = 12^2 + 5^2 - 2(12)(5)\cos(150°)`

       :solmath:`|\vec{u}+\vec{v}|^2 = 169 - 120\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)`

       :solmath:`|\vec{u}+\vec{v}|^2 = 169 + 60\sqrt{3}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`|\vec{u}+\vec{v}| = \sqrt{169+60\sqrt{3}}`

       :solmath:`\text{unit vector} = \dfrac{1}{|\vec{u}+\vec{v}|}(\vec{u}+\vec{v})`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\text{unit vector} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{169+60\sqrt{3}}}(\vec{u}+\vec{v})`
     - **b)** :math:`|3\vec{u} + 2\vec{v}|`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :solmath:`|3\vec{u}+2\vec{v}|^2 = 36^2 + 10^2 - 2(36)(10)\cos(150°)`

       :solmath:`|3\vec{u}+2\vec{v}|^2 = 1396 - 720\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)`

       :solmath:`|3\vec{u}+2\vec{v}|^2 = 1396 + 360\sqrt{3}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`|3\vec{u}+2\vec{v}| = \sqrt{1396+360\sqrt{3}}`

**9)** An airplane is flying with airspeed :math:`400` km/h on a heading of :math:`000°`. There is a
:math:`50` km/h wind blowing from the direction :math:`090°`. Calculate the ground velocity of the plane.

.. rst-class:: solution

The wind (from :math:`090°`, i.e. blowing toward :math:`270°`) is perpendicular to the plane's heading, so
the ground velocity triangle is right-angled:

:solmath:`|\vec{v}|^2 = 400^2 + 50^2`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`|\vec{v}| \approx 403.1 \text{ km/h}`

:solmath:`\theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{50}{400}\right)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\theta \approx 7.1°`

.. rst-class:: solution

The ground velocity is :math:`403.1` km/h at :math:`N7.1°W`.

**10)** An object weighing :math:`400` N is hanging from two ropes attached to the ceiling. One rope makes an
angle of :math:`40°` with the ceiling, the other makes an angle of :math:`50°` with the ceiling. Determine the
tension in each rope.

.. rst-class:: solution

The two ropes and the vertical :math:`400` N weight form a force triangle with angles :math:`40°`,
:math:`50°`, and :math:`90°`:

:solmath:`\sin(40°) = \dfrac{|\vec{T_1}|}{400}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`|\vec{T_1}| \approx 257.1` N (the rope at :math:`40°` to the ceiling)

:solmath:`\cos(40°) = \dfrac{|\vec{T_2}|}{400}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`|\vec{T_2}| \approx 306.4` N (the rope at :math:`50°` to the ceiling)

**11)** A car is moving north at a speed of :math:`25` m/s. A child in the back seat of the car throws a toy
to the passenger in the front seat with a speed of :math:`10` m/s in the direction forward :math:`50°` right
(relative to the car). Calculate the speed and direction of the toy relative to the road.

.. rst-class:: solution

The angle between the car's velocity (:math:`25` m/s) and the toy's velocity relative to the car
(:math:`10` m/s, :math:`50°` off forward) is :math:`50°`, so the angle inside the resultant triangle is
:math:`130°`:

:solmath:`|\vec{r}|^2 = 25^2 + 10^2 - 2(25)(10)\cos(130°)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`|\vec{r}| \approx 32.35` m/s

:solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{10^2-25^2-32.35^2}{-2(25)(32.35)}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\theta \approx 13.7°`

.. rst-class:: solution

The toy's speed is :math:`32.35` m/s with a direction of :math:`N13.7°E` (forward :math:`13.7°` right,
relative to the road).

**12)** A sign weighing :math:`98` N is suspended from the middle of a :math:`4` m long chain. The ends of the
chain are attached to a ceiling at points :math:`3` m apart. Determine the tensions in the chains.

.. rst-class:: solution

Each half of the chain is :math:`2` m long, and the two halves together with the :math:`3` m ceiling span
form an isosceles triangle:

:solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{2^2-3^2-2^2}{-2(3)(2)}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\theta \approx 41.41°` (the base angle at each end of the chain)

.. rst-class:: solution

By symmetry, the two chain tensions are equal. Using the force triangle (base angles :math:`48.59°`,
apex angles :math:`41.41°`):

:solmath:`\dfrac{|\vec{T_2}|}{\sin(48.59°)} = \dfrac{|\vec{T_1}|}{\sin(48.59°)} = \dfrac{98}{\sin(82.82°)}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`|\vec{T_1}| = |\vec{T_2}| \approx 74.1` N

**13)** A canoeist leaves a dock and paddles her canoe at an angle across a river. The current is flowing at
:math:`3` km/h. The resulting velocity of the boat is :math:`5.4` km/h downstream, in a direction that forms
a :math:`15°` angle with the adjacent shore. Determine the canoeist's velocity relative to the water.

.. rst-class:: solution

The paddling vector, the current (:math:`3` km/h along the shore), and the resultant (:math:`5.4` km/h at
:math:`15°` to the shore) form a triangle with a :math:`15°` angle between the current and the resultant:

:solmath:`|\vec{v}|^2 = 3^2 + 5.4^2 - 2(3)(5.4)\cos(15°)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`|\vec{v}| \approx 2.62` km/h

:solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{3^2-5.4^2-2.62^2}{-2(5.4)(2.62)}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\theta \approx 17.24°` (the angle between the paddling vector and the resultant)

.. rst-class:: solution

Measured from the shore, the paddling direction is :math:`15° + 17.24° \approx 32.24°`. The velocity
relative to the water is :math:`2.62` km/h at an angle of :math:`32.24°` with the adjacent shore.

**14)** A pilot flies with a heading of :math:`160°` and an airspeed of :math:`250` km/h. There is a steady
wind of :math:`30` km/h from the direction :math:`030°`. Calculate the ground velocity of the plane.

.. rst-class:: solution

The wind blows toward :math:`210°`; the angle between the heading (:math:`160°`) and the wind direction
(:math:`210°`) is :math:`50°`, giving an interior angle of :math:`130°` in the velocity triangle:

:solmath:`|\vec{v}|^2 = 250^2 + 30^2 - 2(250)(30)\cos(130°)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`|\vec{v}| \approx 270.26` km/h

:solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{30^2-250^2-270.26^2}{-2(250)(270.26)}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\theta \approx 4.88°`

.. rst-class:: solution

The ground velocity is :math:`270.26` km/h at a heading of :math:`S15.12°E`.

**15)** A pilot flies on a heading of :math:`N40°W` with an airspeed of :math:`240` km/h. Her actual ground
velocity is :math:`250` km/h at a bearing of :math:`N42°W`. Determine the velocity of the wind.

.. rst-class:: solution

The angle between the airspeed vector and the ground velocity vector is :math:`2°` (the difference between
the two bearings):

:solmath:`|\vec{w}|^2 = 250^2 + 240^2 - 2(250)(240)\cos(2°)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`|\vec{w}| \approx 13.16` km/h

:solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{250^2-240^2-13.16^2}{-2(240)(13.16)}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\theta \approx 138.94°`

.. rst-class:: solution

Since the ground-velocity bearing is :math:`N42°W` (i.e. :math:`130°` measured the same way as
:math:`\theta`), the wind's bearing is offset from it by :math:`\beta = 138.94° - 130° \approx 8.94°`. The
wind has a velocity of :math:`13.16` km/h at a heading of :math:`N81.1°W`.

**17)** A :math:`500` N crate is resting on a ramp that is inclined :math:`8°` counterclockwise from the
horizontal. Resolve the weight into two rectangular components, one parallel to the ramp and the other
perpendicular to the ramp.

.. rst-class:: solution

Relative to the ramp, the :math:`500` N weight makes an angle of :math:`8°` with the perpendicular to the
ramp:

:solmath:`|\vec{f}| = 500\sin(8°) \approx 69.59 \text{ N (parallel to the ramp)}`

:solmath:`|\vec{n}| = 500\cos(8°) \approx 495.13 \text{ N (perpendicular to the ramp)}`

**18)** A person is pushing on the handle of a lawn mower with a force of :math:`400` N acting :math:`30°`
clockwise below the horizontal. What is the magnitude of the force pushing the lawn mower horizontally?
Pushing down on the lawn mower?

.. rst-class:: solution

:solmath:`|\vec{F_x}| = 400\cos(30°) \approx 346.41 \text{ N (horizontal)}`

:solmath:`|\vec{F_y}| = 400\sin(30°) = 200 \text{ N (downward)}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**19)** A plane on takeoff has a velocity of :math:`300` km/h at an angle of :math:`10°` up from the
horizontal. Calculate the rate at which the plane is climbing and its horizontal speed, to one decimal place.

.. rst-class:: solution

:solmath:`\text{horizontal speed} = 300\cos(10°) \approx 295.4 \text{ km/h}`

:solmath:`\text{rate of climbing} = 300\sin(10°) \approx 52.1 \text{ km/h}`
