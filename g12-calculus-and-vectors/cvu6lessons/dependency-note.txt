This unit is self contained.

Earlier drafts of several lessons referred to visualizations hosted at
https://www.geogebra.org/3d/ with no local equivalent, so understanding
those lessons depended on that external site remaining available.

That gap has been closed: every visualization needed to follow the
lessons is now rendered locally as a gnuplot figure and embedded
directly in the lesson/solutions PDFs, just like every other figure in
this repository. The GeoGebra links remain in the lesson text, but only
as an optional bonus for students who want to explore the scene
interactively (rotate it, inspect it from another angle, etc.) --
they are not required to understand or complete the lesson.
