Answer Key
================================================================================

**1)** a) N65°E  b) S60°E  c) S55°W  d) W  e) N50°W  f) N17°E

**2)** a) 40°  b) 115°  c) 212°  d) 333°  e) 234°  f) 349°

**3)** Diagrams may vary. For example, :math:`\vec{IJ}` is parallel to
:math:`\vec{AB}`, :math:`\vec{KL}` is opposite to :math:`\vec{AB}`,
and :math:`\vec{MN}` is equivalent to :math:`\vec{AB}`.

**4)** a) :math:`\vec{AB} = \vec{ED}, \vec{BC} = \vec{FE}, \vec{CD} = \vec{AF},
\vec{DE} = \vec{BA}, \vec{EF} = \vec{CB}, \vec{FA} = \vec{DC},
\vec{FB} = \vec{EC}, \vec{BF} = \vec{CE}`

b) :math:`\vec{AB} = \vec{DC}, \vec{BA} = \vec{CD}, \vec{AD} = \vec{BC},
\vec{DA} = \vec{CB}, \vec{DE} = \vec{EB}, \vec{BE} = \vec{ED},
\vec{AE} = \vec{EC}, \vec{CE} = \vec{EA}`

**5)** a) 300 km south  b) 700 N on a bearing of 300°
c) 180 km/h on a quadrant bearing of N25°E

**6)** Diagrams may vary.

**7)** a) Vector |---| weight is due to the force of gravity and
therefore has a direction

b\) Scalar |---| this has no direction

c\) Scalar |---| there is no direction so this is just speed

d\) Vector |---| there is magnitude and direction (up)

**8)** a) :math:`\vec{EF}, \vec{IJ}, \vec{KL}, \vec{GH}`
b) :math:`\vec{EF}`  c) :math:`\vec{GH}`
