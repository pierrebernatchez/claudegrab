**7\)** State whether the following are vectors or scalars:

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) A table weighs 80 N

.. rst-class:: solution

Vector |---| weight is due to the force of gravity and therefore has a
direction

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) A woman's age is 60 years old

.. rst-class:: solution

Scalar |---| this has no direction

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) 300 km/h

.. rst-class:: solution

Scalar |---| there is no direction so this is just speed

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

d\) An elevator lifts a person 20 m

.. rst-class:: solution

Vector |---| there is magnitude and direction (up)
