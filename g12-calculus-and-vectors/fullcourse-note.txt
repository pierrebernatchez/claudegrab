This note is to clarify the tasks needed for the full course unit of calculus and vectors course.

We need to complete the additional fullcourse material by deriving it from our upstream cvfc files.

cvfcreview01.pdf has material for the firtst three units, so does
cvfcreview01-solutions.pdf.

So we produce cvfcreview01-en.rst, and cvfcreview01-solutions-en.rst
accordingly.

cvfcreview04.pdf, cvfcreview05.pdf, and cvfcreview06.pdf are review blanks for  units 4,5, and 6.
Use these to create cvfcreview04-en.rst, cvfcreview05-en.rst and cvfcreview06-en.rst

cvfcreview0456-solutions.pdf contains solutions for all three of those blanks.

For the sake of consistency we will split that source to create
cvfcreview04-solutions-en.rst, cvfcreview05-solutions-en.rst, and cvfcreview06-solutions-en.rst.

Finally we have a practise exam document, one without a solutions equivalent.

We produce both the blank and the solutions document from that by working out the solutions
for ourselves: cvfcexam07-en.rst and cvfcexam07-solutions-en.rst.

Once the above is done and reviewed we translate all the cvfc material to french.

This will complete the course.
