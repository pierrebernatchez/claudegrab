   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

       :solmath:`W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s}`

**3\)** Determine the angle between the vectors in each pair.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - a\) :math:`\vec{p} = [6, 7]` and :math:`\vec{q} = [3, 2]`
     - b\) :math:`\vec{r} = [-1, -7]` and :math:`\vec{s} = [5, 4]`
   * - :solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{\vec{p}\cdot\vec{q}}{|\vec{p}||\vec{q}|}`

       :solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{6(3)+7(2)}{\left(\sqrt{(6)^2+(7)^2}\right)\left(\sqrt{(3)^2+(2)^2}\right)}`

       :solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{32}{(\sqrt{85})(\sqrt{13})}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\theta \cong 15.71°`
     - :solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{\vec{r}\cdot\vec{s}}{|\vec{r}||\vec{s}|}`

       :solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{-1(5)+(-7)(4)}{\left(\sqrt{(-1)^2+(-7)^2}\right)\left(\sqrt{(5)^2+(4)^2}\right)}`

       :solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{-33}{(\sqrt{50})(\sqrt{41})}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\theta \cong 136.79°`

**4\)** Determine the projection of the first vector on the second.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - a\) :math:`\vec{a} = [6, -1], \vec{b} = [3, -4]`

       :solmath:`proj_{\vec{b}}\,\vec{a} = \dfrac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{\vec{b}\cdot\vec{b}}(\vec{b})`

       :solmath:`= \dfrac{6(3)+(-1)(-4)}{3^2+(-4)^2}[3, -4]`

       :solmath:`= \dfrac{22}{25}[3, -4]`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= \left[\dfrac{66}{25}, \dfrac{-88}{25}\right]`
     - b\) :math:`\vec{c} = [6, 7], \vec{d} = [3, 2]`

       :solmath:`proj_{\vec{d}}\,\vec{c} = \dfrac{\vec{c}\cdot\vec{d}}{\vec{d}\cdot\vec{d}}(\vec{d})`

       :solmath:`= \dfrac{6(3)+7(2)}{3^2+2^2}[3, 2]`

       :solmath:`= \dfrac{32}{13}[3, 2]`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= \left[\dfrac{96}{13}, \dfrac{64}{13}\right]`

**6\)** For each of the following, find the magnitude of the
projection of :math:`\vec{x}` on :math:`\vec{y}` and also the vector
projection of :math:`\vec{x}` on :math:`\vec{y}`.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - a\) :math:`\vec{x} = [1, 1], \vec{y} = [1, -1]`

       :solmath:`|proj_{\vec{y}}\,\vec{x}| = \left|\dfrac{\vec{x}\cdot\vec{y}}{|\vec{y}|}\right|`

       :solmath:`= \left|\dfrac{1(1)+1(-1)}{\sqrt{(1)^2+(-1)^2}}\right|`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= 0`

       :solmath:`proj_{\vec{y}}\,\vec{x} = 0\left(\dfrac{\vec{y}}{|\vec{y}|}\right)`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= \vec{0}`
     - b\) :math:`\vec{x} = [2, 5], \vec{y} = [-5, 12]`

       :solmath:`|proj_{\vec{y}}\,\vec{x}| = \left|\dfrac{\vec{x}\cdot\vec{y}}{|\vec{y}|}\right|`

       :solmath:`= \left|\dfrac{2(-5)+5(12)}{\sqrt{(-5)^2+(12)^2}}\right|`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= \dfrac{50}{13}`

       :solmath:`proj_{\vec{y}}\,\vec{x} = \dfrac{50}{13}\left(\dfrac{[-5, 12]}{13}\right)`

       :solmath:`= \dfrac{50}{169}[-5, 12]`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= \left[\dfrac{-250}{169}, \dfrac{600}{169}\right]`

**7\)** :math:`\triangle DEF` has vertices :math:`D(-3, 5), E(2, 3)`,
and :math:`F(6, 7)`. Calculate :math:`\angle DEF`.

:solmath:`\vec{ED} = [-5, 2]`

:solmath:`\vec{EF} = [4, 4]`

:solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{\vec{ED}\cdot\vec{EF}}{|\vec{ED}||\vec{EF}|}`

:solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{-5(4)+2(4)}{(\sqrt{29})(\sqrt{32})}`

:solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{-12}{\sqrt{928}}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\theta \cong 113.2°`

**8\)** How much work is done against gravity by the orderly pushing
an 85 kg person up a 5 m ramp inclined at an angle of 15° to the
horizontal?

.. image:: ../images/cvu5worksheet03-gpimage06.png
   :scale: 85
   :alt: orderly pushing a person up a 5 m ramp inclined at 15 degrees, force 833 N at 75 degrees to the ramp

:solmath:`\vec{F} = 85(9.8) = 833` N

:solmath:`W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s}`

:solmath:`W = 833(5)\cos(75)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`W \cong 1077.98` J

**9\)** A stage lamp is dragged 15 m along level ground by a 120 N
force applied at an angle of 35° to the ground. It is then dragged up
a 12 m ramp onto a stage by the same force. If the ramp is inclined at
15° to the ground. Find the total work done.

:solmath:`W = 120(15)\cos(35°) + 120(12)\cos(20°)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`W \cong 2827.63` J

**10\)** A box on a wagon pulled a distance of 35 m by a 27 N force
applied at an angle of 40° to the ground. The box is then lifted a
distance of 1.5 m and placed on a table by exerting a force of 37 N.
Find the total work done.

:solmath:`W = 27(35)\cos(40°) + 37(1.5)\cos(0)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`W \cong 779.4` J
