**5\)** Let :math:`\vec{a} = [1, -2], \vec{b} = [2, 5]`, and
:math:`\vec{c} = [4, -1]`. Evaluate the following if possible. If not
possible, explain why not.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - a\) :math:`\vec{a} \cdot (\vec{b} + \vec{c})`

       :solmath:`= [1, -2] \cdot ([2, 5] + [4, -1])`

       :solmath:`= [1, -2] \cdot [6, 4]`

       :solmath:`= 1(6) + (-2)(4)`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= -2`
     - b\) :math:`(\vec{a} + \vec{b}) \cdot \vec{c}`

       :solmath:`= ([1, -2] + [2, 5]) \cdot [4, -1]`

       :solmath:`= [3, 3] \cdot [4, -1]`

       :solmath:`= 3(4) + 3(-1)`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= 9`
   * - c\) :math:`(\vec{a} + \vec{b}) \cdot (\vec{a} + \vec{c})`

       :solmath:`= ([1, -2] + [2, 5]) \cdot ([1, -2] + [4, -1])`

       :solmath:`= [3, 3] \cdot [5, -3]`

       :solmath:`= 3(5) + 3(-3)`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= 6`
     - d\) :math:`(3\vec{a} + 2\vec{b}) \cdot (4\vec{a} - \vec{b})`

       :solmath:`= (3[1, -2] + 2[2, 5]) \cdot (4[1, -2] - [2, 5])`

       :solmath:`= ([3, -6] + [4, 10]) \cdot ([4, -8] - [2, 5])`

       :solmath:`= [7, 4] \cdot [2, -13]`

       :solmath:`= 7(2) + 4(-13)`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= -38`
   * - e\) :math:`\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} \cdot \vec{c}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       Not possible. :math:`\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}` is a scalar, and we
       can't dot :math:`\vec{c}` with a scalar.
     - f\) :math:`\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + \vec{a} \cdot \vec{c}`

       :solmath:`= [1, -2] \cdot [2, 5] + [1, -2] \cdot [4, -1]`

       :solmath:`= 1(2) + (-2)(5) + 1(4) + (-2)(-1)`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= -2`
   * - g\) :math:`4\vec{b} \cdot (-2\vec{c})`

       :solmath:`= 4[2, 5] \cdot (-2[4, -1])`

       :solmath:`= [8, 20] \cdot [-8, 2]`

       :solmath:`= 8(-8) + 20(2)`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= -24`
     - h\) :math:`(\vec{a} + \vec{b}) \cdot \vec{c}`

       :solmath:`= ([1, -2] + [2, 5]) \cdot [4, -1]`

       :solmath:`= [3, 3] \cdot [4, -1]`

       :solmath:`= 3(4) + 3(-1)`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= 9`

**6\)** Determine a value of :math:`t` so that :math:`\vec{u} = [9,
t]` and :math:`\vec{v} = [-16, t]` are perpendicular.

If perpendicular, :solmath:`\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 0`

:solmath:`[9, t] \cdot [-16, t] = 0`

:solmath:`9(-16) + t(t) = 0`

:solmath:`t^2 = 144`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`t = \pm 12`

**7\)** Find a vector that is perpendicular to :math:`\vec{a} = [3,
-1]`. Verify that the vectors are perpendicular.

:solmath:`\vec{a} = [3, -1], \vec{b} = [5, k]`

:solmath:`\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 0`

:solmath:`3(5) + (-1)(k) = 0`

:solmath:`k = 15`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\vec{b} = [5, 15]`

Shortcut: to find a vector perpendicular to :math:`[3, -1]`, swap the
components and change one sign: :math:`[3, -1] \to [-1, 3] \to [1,
3]`.

Check: :math:`[1, 3] \cdot [3, -1] = 3 + (-3) = 0`

**8\)** Which of the following is a right-angled triangle? Identify
the right angle in that triangle.

- :math:`\triangle ABC` for :math:`A(3, 1), B(-2, 3)`, and
  :math:`C(5, 6)`
- :math:`\triangle STU` for :math:`S(4, 6), T(-3, 7)`, and
  :math:`U(-5, -4)`

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - :solmath:`\vec{AB} = [-5, 2]`

       :solmath:`\vec{BC} = [7, 3]`

       :solmath:`\vec{AC} = [2, 5]`

       :solmath:`\vec{AB} \cdot \vec{BC} = -5(7) + 2(3) = -35 + 6 = -29`

       :solmath:`\vec{AB} \cdot \vec{AC} = -5(2) + 2(5) = 0`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       So :math:`\vec{AB}` and :math:`\vec{AC}` are perpendicular.
       :math:`\angle A = 90°`
     - :solmath:`\vec{ST} = [-7, 1]`

       :solmath:`\vec{SU} = [-9, -10]`

       :solmath:`\vec{TU} = [-2, -11]`

       :solmath:`\vec{ST} \cdot \vec{SU} = -7(-9) + 1(-10) = 53`

       :solmath:`\vec{ST} \cdot \vec{TU} = -7(-2) + 1(-11) = 3`

       :solmath:`\vec{SU} \cdot \vec{TU} = -9(-2) + (-10)(-11) = 128`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       So :math:`\triangle STU` is NOT a right triangle.
