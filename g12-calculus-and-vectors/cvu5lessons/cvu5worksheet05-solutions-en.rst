**3\)** Find a unit vector perpendicular to :math:`\vec{a}= [6, -2,
-3]` and :math:`\vec{b} = [5, 1, -4]`.

:solmath:`\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = [-2(-4)-(-3)(1), -3(5)-6(-4),
6(1)-(-2)(5)]`

:solmath:`\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = [11, 9, 16]`

:solmath:`|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}| = \sqrt{11^2+9^2+16^2} = \sqrt{458}`

:solmath:`\text{unit vector} = \dfrac{1}{|\vec{a}\times\vec{b}|}
[\vec{a}\times\vec{b}]`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\text{unit vector} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{458}}[11, 9, 16]`

**5\)** Use the cross product to determine the angles between the
vectors :math:`\vec{a} = [2, 1, -3]` and :math:`\vec{b} = [5, -4, 3]`.
Consider ambiguous case. Use dot product to confirm or use graphing
software to inspect.

:solmath:`\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = [1(3)-(-3)(-4), -3(5)-2(3),
2(-4)-1(5)]`

:solmath:`\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = [-9, -21, -13]`

:solmath:`\sin\theta = \dfrac{|\vec{a}\times\vec{b}|}{|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|}
= \dfrac{\sqrt{691}}{(\sqrt{14})(\sqrt{50})}`

:solmath:`\theta_1 \cong 83.5°`

:solmath:`\theta_2 = 180° - \theta_1 \cong 96.5°`

:solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|}
= \dfrac{2(5)+1(-4)+(-3)(3)}{(\sqrt{14})(\sqrt{50})} =
\dfrac{-3}{\sqrt{700}}`

:solmath:`\theta \cong 96.5°`, verified using the dot product.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\theta \cong 96.5°`

**6\)** Determine the area of :math:`\triangle PQR` with vertices of
:math:`P(3, -2, 7)`, :math:`Q(2, 2, -3)`, and :math:`R(1, 1, 2)`.

:solmath:`\vec{PQ} = [2-3, 2-(-2), -3-7] = [-1, 4, -10]`

:solmath:`\vec{PR} = [1-3, 1-(-2), 2-7] = [-2, 3, -5]`

:solmath:`\vec{PQ} \times \vec{PR} = [4(-5)-(-10)(3), -10(-2)-(-1)(-5),
-1(3)-4(-2)]`

:solmath:`\vec{PQ} \times \vec{PR} = [10, 15, 5]`

:solmath:`\text{Area of }\triangle PQR = \dfrac{1}{2}|\vec{PQ} \times
\vec{PR}| = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{350} = \dfrac{1}{2}(5)\sqrt{14}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\text{Area of }\triangle PQR = 2.5\sqrt{14}` units\ :superscript:`2`
