Answer Key
================================================================================

**1)** a) :math:`\lambda \cong 0.031` /min  b) 22 min
c) :math:`N(t) = 100\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{t}{22}}`
d) :math:`\cong -2.7` mg/min

**2)** a) i) 83.3 mg  ii) 27.9 mg
b) i) -15.2 mg/day  ii) -5.1 mg/day
c) 7.6 days

**3)** :math:`t = 0` s, :math:`v = 1` m/s

**4)** a) He will NOT be able to rescue Rocco.
b) He must swing back and forth 3.75 times before he can safely drop
to the ground.

**5)** a) max voltage: 170 V at times :math:`t`, in seconds,
:math:`t = \left\{\dfrac{4k+1}{240}, k \in \mathbb{Z}, k \geq 0\right\}`;
min voltage: :math:`-170` V at times :math:`t`, in seconds,
:math:`t = \left\{\dfrac{4k+3}{240}, k \in \mathbb{Z}, k \geq 0\right\}`

b) i) :math:`T = \dfrac{1}{60}` s  ii) :math:`f = 60` Hz
iii) :math:`A = 170` V

**6)** a) 1.42 s  b) :math:`h(t) = 8\cos(4.43t)`
c) :math:`v(t) = -35.44\sin(4.43t)`  d) :math:`a(t) = -157\cos(4.43t)`
e) max velocity: 35.4 cm/s at time :math:`t = 1.06` s

**7)** a) :math:`v(t) = -0.65\sin(13t)`
b) max velocity: 0.65 m/s at
:math:`t = \left\{\dfrac{(4k+3)\pi}{26}, k \in \mathbb{Z}, k \geq 0\right\}`;
min velocity: :math:`-0.65` m/s at
:math:`t = \left\{\dfrac{(4k+1)\pi}{26}, k \in \mathbb{Z}, k \geq 0\right\}`
