opposite bank.

Answer Key
================================================================================

**1)** 806.2 km/h :math:`N7.1°E`  **2)** 15.93 units :math:`N57.8°E`
**3)** 24 cm string: 39.2 N, 32 cm string: 29.4 N

**4)** 45° rope: 175.73 N  30° rope: 143.48 N

**5)** a) 0.6 km downstream from the marina  b) 6 minutes (0.1 hours)
c) upstream 17.5°, resultant velocity: 19.1 km/h

**6)** 60°  **7)** 108.1 km/h :math:`N18.6°W`
