**1\)** The velocity of an airplane is 800 km/h north. A wind is
blowing due east at 100 km/h. Determine the velocity of the airplane
relative to the ground.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - :solmath:`|\vec{v}_{ground}|^2 = 800^2 + 100^2`

       :solmath:`|\vec{v}_{ground}| \approx 806.2` km/h

       :solmath:`\tan\theta = \dfrac{100}{800}`

       :solmath:`\theta \approx 7.1°`
     - .. image:: ../images/cvu4worksheet04-gpimage01.png
          :scale: 85
          :alt: right triangle with 800 km/h vertical, 100 km/h horizontal, and resultant r in red

.. rst-class:: solution

The ground velocity is 806.2 km/h at a quadrant bearing of
:math:`N7.1°E`.

**2\)** A particle is displaced 5 units to the east and then displaced
12 units in a direction :math:`N45°E`. Find the magnitude and direction
of the resultant displacement.

.. image:: ../images/cvu4worksheet04-gpimage02.png
   :scale: 85
   :alt: vector triangle with 5 units east, 12 units at N45E, and resultant r

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - :solmath:`|\vec{r}|^2 = 5^2 + 12^2 - 2(5)(12) \cos(135°)`

       :solmath:`|\vec{r}| \approx 15.93` units

       :solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{12^2 - 5^2 - 15.93^2}{-2(5)(15.93)}`
     - :solmath:`\theta \approx 32.2°`

       :solmath:`\beta = 90 - 32.2°`

       :solmath:`\beta \approx 57.8°`

.. rst-class:: solution

The resultant is 15.93 units at a quadrant bearing of :math:`N57.8°E`.

**3\)** A mass of 5 kg is suspended by two strings, 24 cm and 32 cm
long, from two points that are 40 cm apart and at the same level.
Determine the tension in each of the strings.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 1
   :width: 100%

   * - Position Diagram
     - Vector Diagram
   * - .. image:: ../images/cvu4worksheet04-gpimage03.png
          :scale: 85
     - .. image:: ../images/cvu4worksheet04-gpimage04.png
          :scale: 85

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - :solmath:`\cos\Theta_1 = \dfrac{24^2 - 40^2 - 32^2}{-2(40)(32)}`

       :solmath:`\Theta_1 \approx 36.87°`

       :solmath:`\cos 53.13° = \dfrac{|\vec{T_1}|}{49}`

       :solmath:`|\vec{T_1}| \approx 29.4` N (32 cm string)
     - :solmath:`\cos\Theta_2 = \dfrac{32^2 - 24^2 - 40^2}{-2(24)(40)}`

       :solmath:`\Theta_2 \approx 53.13°`

       :solmath:`\sin 53.13° = \dfrac{|\vec{T_2}|}{49}`

       :solmath:`|\vec{T_2}| \approx 39.2` N (24 cm string)

**4\)** A mass of 20 kg is suspended from a ceiling by two lengths of
rope that make angles of 30° and 45° with the ceiling. Determine the
tension in each rope.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50

   :class: solution

   \dfrac{196}{\sin 75} = \dfrac{|\vec{T_{45}}|}{\sin 60} = \dfrac{|\vec{T_{30}}|}{\sin 45}

**5\)** A river is 2 km wide and flows at 6 km/h. Anna is driving a
motorboat, which has a speed of 20 km/h in still water and she heads
out from one bank in a direction perpendicular to the current. A
marina lies directly across the river from the starting point on the
opposite bank.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) How far downstream from the marina will the current push the boat?

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - :solmath:`\tan 16.7° = \dfrac{x}{2}`

       :solmath:`x \approx 0.6` km
     - .. image:: ../images/cvu4worksheet04-gpimage07.png
          :scale: 85
          :alt: right triangle with 20 km/h vertical, 6 km/h horizontal, and resultant r1 in red

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How long will it take for the boat to cross the river?

.. math::
   :class: solution

   \cos 16.7° = \dfrac{20}{|\vec{r_1}|}

.. math::
   :class: solution

   |\vec{r_1}| \approx 20.9 \text{ km/h}

.. rst-class:: solution

Time to cross :math:`= \dfrac{2.1 \text{ km}}{20.9 \text{ km/h}} \approx 0.1` hours
(6 minutes)

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) If Anna decides that she wants to end up directly across the
river at the marina, in what direction should she head? What is the
resultant velocity of the boat?

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - :solmath:`\sin\theta = \dfrac{6}{20}`

       :solmath:`\theta \approx 17.5°`

       :solmath:`|\vec{r_3}|^2 = 20^2 - 6^2`

       :solmath:`|\vec{r_3}| \approx 19.1 \text{ km/h}`
     - .. image:: ../images/cvu4worksheet04-gpimage08.png
          :scale: 85
          :alt: right triangle with 20 km/h as hypotenuse, 6 km/h horizontal, and r3 vertical in red

.. rst-class:: solution

The boat needs to head out at an angle of 72.5° with the shore. The
resultant velocity is 19.1 km/h.

**6\)** Adam can swim at the rate of 2 km/h in still water. At what
angle to the bank of a river must he head if he wants to swim directly
across the river and the current in the river moves at the rate of
1 km/h.

.. image:: ../images/cvu4worksheet04-gpimage09.png
   :scale: 85
   :alt: right triangle with 2 km/h hypotenuse, 1 km/h horizontal, and r vertical in red

.. math::
   :class: solution

   \sin\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}

.. math::
   :class: solution

   \theta = 30°

.. rst-class:: solution

Adam must head at 60° to the bank of the river.

**7\)** An airplane is travelling :math:`N60°E` with a resultant ground
speed of 205 km/h. The nose of the plane is actually pointing east with
an airspeed of 212 km/h. Find the wind speed and direction.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - :solmath:`|\vec{w}|^2 = 205^2 + 212^2 - 2(205)(212) \cos(30)`

       :solmath:`|\vec{w}| \approx 108.1` km/h

       :solmath:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{205^2 - 212^2 - 108.1^2}{-2(212)(108.1)}`

       :solmath:`\theta \approx 71.4°`

       :solmath:`\beta \approx 18.6°`
     - .. image:: ../images/cvu4worksheet04-gpimage10.png
          :scale: 85
          :alt: triangle with 212 km/h horizontal, 205 km/h at 30 degrees, and wind w in red

.. rst-class:: solution

The wind speed is 108.1 km/h at a quadrant bearing of :math:`N18.6°W`.
