**21\)** A canoeist leaves a dock and paddles her canoe at an angle

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Draw a vector diagram of this situation.

.. rst-class:: solution

.. image:: ../images/cvu4review06-gpimage37.png
   :scale: 85
   :alt: canoe velocity v and current 3 km/h summing to the 5.4 km/h resultant at 15 degrees from shore

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) Determine the canoeist's velocity relative to the water.

.. math::
   :class: solution

   |\vec{u}|^2 = 5.4^2 + 3^2 - 2(5.4)(3)\cos(15°)

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`|\vec{v}| \approx 2.6` km/h at an angle of 32.2° with the
adjacent shore.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) How far downstream will she be in 20 min?

.. math::
   :class: solution

   d = 5.4 \times \dfrac{20}{60} = 1.8 \text{ km}

.. math::
   :class: solution

   \cos(15°) = \dfrac{x}{1.8}

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x \approx 1.7` km

**26\)** A plane is headed :math:`N10°W` with a speed of 600 km/h. An
east wind (i.e. wind FROM the east towards the west) causes it to
travel at :math:`N16°W`. Find the resultant ground speed of the
plane, and the speed of the wind, each to 1 decimal place.

   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

       :math:`|\vec{w}| \approx 65.2` km/h (wind speed)

Section 27: 4.5 -- Resultant from Components
================================================================================

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**27\)** The force at which a tow truck pulls a car has a horizontal
component of 20 000 N and a vertical component of 12 000 N. What is
the resultant force on the car?

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :header-rows: 0
   :width: 100%

   * - :solmath:`|\vec{F}|^2 = 12000^2 + 20000^2`

       :solmath:`|\vec{F}| \approx 23\,323.8` N

       :solmath:`\tan\theta = \dfrac{12000}{20000}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\theta \approx 31°`
     - .. image:: ../images/cvu4review06-gpimage44.png
          :scale: 85
          :alt: Fx 20000 N and Fy 12000 N resolved into resultant F

.. rst-class:: solution

A force of 23 323.8 N at an angle of 31° with the horizontal.
