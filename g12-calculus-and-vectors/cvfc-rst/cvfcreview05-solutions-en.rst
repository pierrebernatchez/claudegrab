**12)** Determine if :math:`\triangle PQR` with vertices :math:`P(-5,3)`, :math:`Q(-7,8)` and :math:`R(3,12)` is
a right triangle. If so, identify the right angle.

.. image:: ../images/cvfcreview05-gpimage05.png
   :scale: 85
   :alt: triangle PQR with vertices P(-5,3), Q(-7,8), R(3,12)

.. rst-class:: solution

:solmath:`\vec{PQ} = [-2,5], |\vec{PQ}| = \sqrt{29}`

:solmath:`\vec{PR} = [8,9], |\vec{PR}| = \sqrt{145}`

:solmath:`\vec{RQ} = [-10,-4], |\vec{RQ}| = \sqrt{116}`

.. rst-class:: solution

Check the Pythagorean theorem against the longest side, :math:`\vec{PR}`:

:solmath:`\left(\sqrt{29}\right)^2 + \left(\sqrt{116}\right)^2 \overset{?}{=} \left(\sqrt{145}\right)^2`

:solmath:`29 + 116 = 145`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`145 = 145` so :math:`\triangle PQR` is a right triangle, with the right angle at :math:`Q`.

**24)** A storage box is dragged :math:`7` m along a smooth, level floor by a :math:`43`-N force, applied at
:math:`38°` to the floor. Then, it is pulled :math:`3.5` m up a ramp inclined at :math:`24°` to the horizontal,
using the same force. The storage box is then dragged a further :math:`6` m along a level platform using the
same force again. Determine the total work done in moving the storage box.

.. rst-class:: solution

On the level floor and the level platform, the angle between the force and the direction of motion stays
:math:`38°`. On the ramp, the angle between the force and the direction of motion (along the ramp) is
:math:`38° - 24° = 14°`:

:solmath:`W = 7(43)\cos(38°) + 3.5(43)\cos(14°) + 6(43)\cos(38°)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`W \approx 586.5` J
