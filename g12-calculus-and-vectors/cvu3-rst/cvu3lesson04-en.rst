Part 1: Derivatives of Exponential Functions
================================================================================

**Chain Rule:**

If :math:`h(x) = f(g(x))`

:math:`h'(x) = f'[g(x)] \times g'(x)`

**Apply to exponential functions:**

If :math:`h(x) = b^{g(x)}`

:math:`h'(x) = b^{g(x)} \times \ln b \times g'(x)`
