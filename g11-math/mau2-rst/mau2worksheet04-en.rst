Answers
================================================================================

**1)** a) :math:`\dfrac{7x+3}{90}`, no restrictions
b) :math:`\dfrac{5}{12x}`, :math:`x \neq 0`
c) :math:`\dfrac{12+5a}{4ab}`, :math:`a \neq 0,\; b \neq 0`
d) :math:`\dfrac{6b+3ab+4a-a^2}{3a^2b^2}`, :math:`a \neq 0,\; b \neq 0`

**2)** a) :math:`\dfrac{12}{(x-6)(x+6)}`, :math:`x \neq -6, 6`
b) :math:`\dfrac{15x-84}{(x+8)(x-9)}`, :math:`x \neq -8, 9`
c) :math:`\dfrac{23x+22}{(x-6)(x+4)}`, :math:`x \neq -4, 6`
d) :math:`\dfrac{2(x+4)(x-1)}{(x+1)(x-2)}`, :math:`x \neq -1, 2`

**3)** a) :math:`\dfrac{3x-2}{(x-1)(x-8)}`, :math:`x \neq 1, 8`
b) :math:`\dfrac{x^2+2x-4}{(x+5)(x-2)}`, :math:`x \neq -5, 2`
c) :math:`\dfrac{-2x^2+3x+4}{(x+1)(x+2)(x+7)}`, :math:`x \neq -7, -2, -1`
d) :math:`\dfrac{-(x-23)(x-1)}{(x+11)(x-11)(x-3)}`, :math:`x \neq -11, 3, 11`

**4)** a) :math:`\dfrac{2}{x-2}`, :math:`x \neq 2`
b) :math:`\dfrac{b-3}{4b-1}`, :math:`b \neq \dfrac{1}{4}`
