**5)** Simplify. Express your answers using only positive exponents.

   :widths: 33 33 34
   :width: 100%

   * - a\) :math:`x^{\frac{1}{4}}\cdot x^{\frac{1}{4}}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

     - b\) :math:`\left(m^{\frac{1}{3}}\right)\left(m^{\frac{3}{4}}\right)`

     - e\) :math:`\left(y^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^{\frac{2}{3}}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`=y^{\frac{2}{6}}=y^{\frac{1}{3}}`
     - f\) :math:`\left(u^{\frac{3}{4}}v^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^{\frac{2}{9}}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`=u^{\frac{6}{36}}v^{\frac{2}{18}}=u^{\frac{1}{6}}v^{\frac{1}{9}}`

**6)** Simplify. Express your answers using only positive exponents.

   * - d\) :math:`\left(w^{-\frac{8}{9}}\right)^{-\frac{3}{4}}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`=w^{\frac{24}{36}}=w^{\frac{2}{3}}`
     - e\) :math:`(8x)^{\frac{2}{3}}(27x)^{-\frac{1}{3}}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`=4x^{\frac{2}{3}}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}x^{-\frac{1}{3}}=\dfrac{4}{3}x^{\frac{1}{3}}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`=5\cdot7^{-2}y^{\frac{4}{3}}=\dfrac{5}{49}y^{\frac{4}{3}}`

**7)** The surface area, S, of a sphere can be expressed in terms of its
volume, V, using the formula :math:`S(V) = (4\pi)^{\frac{1}{3}}(3V)^{\frac{2}{3}}`.
A beach ball has a volume of 24 000 cm³. Find its surface area, to the
nearest hundred square centimeters.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S(24\,000) = (4\pi)^{\frac{1}{3}}[3(24\,000)]^{\frac{2}{3}} =
(4\pi)^{\frac{1}{3}}(72\,000)^{\frac{2}{3}}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\approx 4023.7` square centimeters, which rounds to 4000 square
centimeters.
