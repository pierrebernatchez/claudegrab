Fractions Review
================================================================================

Adding and Subtracting Fractions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*Always find a common denominator when adding or subtracting fractions!*

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{8}`

|nbsp|

|nbsp|

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`\dfrac{2x}{3y}-\dfrac{y}{2}`

|nbsp|

|nbsp|

|nbsp|

Multiplying and Dividing Fractions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*You do NOT need a common denominator when multiplying or dividing
fractions!*

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}`

|nbsp|

|nbsp|

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`\dfrac{2}{3}\div\dfrac{4}{3}`

|nbsp|

|nbsp|

**Rule:** We can NEVER have a fraction with a denominator of 0. Why?

|nbsp|

|nbsp|

**Rule:** Cross multiplication of fractions only happens when there is an
:math:`=` sign between two fractions.

|nbsp|

|nbsp|

**Rule:** We can cancel out ONLY when multiplying fractions

|nbsp|

|nbsp|

**Rule:** We can NOT cancel out when adding or subtracting fractions

|nbsp|

|nbsp|
