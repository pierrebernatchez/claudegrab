**7)** The surface area, S, of a sphere can be expressed in terms of its
volume, V, using the formula :math:`S(V) = (4\pi)^{\frac{1}{3}}(3V)^{\frac{2}{3}}`.
A beach ball has a volume of 24 000 cm³. Find its surface area, to the
nearest hundred square centimeters.

|nbsp|

|nbsp|

|nbsp|
