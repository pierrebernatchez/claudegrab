.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**1)** Describe the transformations, in order, that are being done to the
function :math:`f(x)`.

.. container:: keeptogether

   a\) :math:`g(x)=-4f(x)`

   .. rst-class:: solution

   vertical reflection over the x-axis (:math:`-y`); vertical stretch by a
   factor of 4 (:math:`4y`)

.. container:: keeptogether

   b\) :math:`g(x)=f(3x)`

   .. rst-class:: solution

   horizontal compression by a factor of :math:`\dfrac{1}{3}`
   (:math:`\dfrac{x}{3}`)

.. container:: keeptogether

   c\) :math:`g(x)=\dfrac{1}{2}f(-x)`

   .. rst-class:: solution

   vertical compression by a factor of :math:`\dfrac{1}{2}`
   (:math:`\dfrac{y}{2}`); horizontal reflection over the y-axis
   (:math:`-x`)

.. container:: keeptogether

   d\) :math:`g(x)=-\dfrac{1}{3}f\left[\dfrac{1}{2}(x+1)\right]`

   .. rst-class:: solution

   vertical reflection over the x-axis and vertical compression by a factor
   of :math:`\dfrac{1}{3}` (:math:`\dfrac{y}{-3}`); horizontal stretch by a
   factor of 2 (:math:`2x`); phase shift 1 unit left (:math:`x-1`)

.. container:: keeptogether

   e\) :math:`g(x)=5f[-2(x-4)]`

   .. rst-class:: solution

   vertical stretch by a factor of 5 (:math:`5y`); horizontal reflection
   over the y-axis and horizontal compression by a factor of
   :math:`\dfrac{1}{2}` (:math:`\dfrac{x}{-2}`); phase shift 4 units right
   (:math:`x+4`)

.. container:: keeptogether

   f\) :math:`g(x)=-2f(8x)+4`

   .. rst-class:: solution

   vertical reflection over the x-axis and vertical stretch by a factor of 2
   (:math:`-2y`); horizontal compression by a factor of :math:`\dfrac{1}{8}`
   (:math:`\dfrac{x}{8}`); shift up 4 units (:math:`y+4`)

.. container:: keeptogether

   h\) :math:`g(x)=-\dfrac{1}{4}f[-3(x-1)]-5`

   .. rst-class:: solution

   vertical reflection over the x-axis and vertical compression by a factor
   of :math:`\dfrac{1}{4}` (:math:`\dfrac{y}{-4}`); horizontal reflection
   over the y-axis and horizontal compression by a factor of
   :math:`\dfrac{1}{3}` (:math:`\dfrac{x}{-3}`); phase shift 1 unit right
   (:math:`x+1`); shift down 5 units (:math:`y-5`)

.. container:: keeptogether

   i\) :math:`g(x)=4f\left[-\dfrac{1}{2}(x+2)\right]-1`

   .. rst-class:: solution

   vertical stretch by a factor of 4 (:math:`4y`); horizontal reflection
   over the y-axis and horizontal stretch by a factor of 2 (:math:`-2x`);
   phase shift 2 units left (:math:`x-2`); shift down 1 unit (:math:`y-1`)

Answers
================================================================================

**1)** a) vertical reflection over the x-axis and vertical stretch by a
factor of 4 :math:`(-4y)`

b) horizontal compression by a factor of :math:`\dfrac{1}{3}`
:math:`\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)`

c) vertical compression by a factor of :math:`\dfrac{1}{2}`
:math:`\left(\dfrac{y}{2}\right)`, horizontal reflection over the y-axis
(:math:`-x`)

d) vertical reflection over the x-axis and vertical compression by a factor
of :math:`\dfrac{1}{3}` :math:`\left(\dfrac{y}{-3}\right)`, horizontal
stretch by a factor of 2 (:math:`2x`), phase shift left 1 unit
(:math:`x-1`)

e) vertical stretch by a factor of 5 (:math:`5y`), horizontal reflection
over the y-axis and horizontal compression by a factor of
:math:`\dfrac{1}{2}` :math:`\left(\dfrac{x}{-2}\right)`, phase shift right 4
units (:math:`x+4`)

f) vertical reflection over the x-axis and vertical stretch by a factor of 2
(:math:`-2y`), horizontal compression by a factor of :math:`\dfrac{1}{8}`
:math:`\left(\dfrac{x}{8}\right)`, shift up 4 units (:math:`y+4`)

h) vertical reflection over the x-axis and vertical compression by a factor
of :math:`\dfrac{1}{4}` :math:`\left(\dfrac{y}{-4}\right)`, horizontal
reflection over the y-axis and horizontal compression by a factor of
:math:`\dfrac{1}{3}` :math:`\left(\dfrac{x}{-3}\right)`, phase shift right 1
unit (:math:`x+1`), shift down 5 units (:math:`y-5`)

i) vertical stretch by a factor of 4 (:math:`4y`), horizontal reflection
over the y-axis and horizontal stretch by a factor of 2 (:math:`-2x`),
phase shift left 2 units (:math:`x-2`), shift down 1 unit (:math:`y-1`)
