**9)** Find the inverse, :math:`f^{-1}(x)`, algebraically if
:math:`f(x) = -2\sqrt{x+1} - 5`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x = -2\sqrt{y+1}-5 \Rightarrow \dfrac{x+5}{-2} = \sqrt{y+1}
\Rightarrow \left(\dfrac{x+5}{-2}\right)^2 = y+1`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`f^{-1}(x) = \left(\dfrac{x+5}{-2}\right)^2 - 1`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**10)** Find the inverse, :math:`f^{-1}(x)`, algebraically if
:math:`f(x) = \dfrac{1}{3}(x-4)^2 + 2`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x = \dfrac{1}{3}(y-4)^2+2 \Rightarrow x-2 = \dfrac{1}{3}(y-4)^2
\Rightarrow 3x-6 = (y-4)^2 \Rightarrow \pm\sqrt{3x-6} = y-4`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`f^{-1}(x) = \pm\sqrt{3x-6} + 4` (equivalently
:math:`\pm\sqrt{3(x-2)} + 4`)

e) shift down 3 units

3 units. b) horizontal compression BAFO :math:`\dfrac{1}{3}` and then
shift up 2 units. c) vertical stretch BAFO 3 and then horizontal
reflection in the :math:`y`-axis.

**3)** vertical stretch BAFO 3, then shift right 2 units and down 11
units.

**4)** vertical stretch by a factor of 2, then shift right 2 units and
down 9 units.

**5)** see graphs above

reflection in the :math:`y`-axis

**7)** see solutions for 7 & 8 above

**9)** :math:`f^{-1}(x) = \left(\dfrac{x+5}{-2}\right)^2 - 1`
