.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :width: 100%

   * - **Arithmetic**

       Sequence: :math:`t_n = a+(n-1)d`

       Series: :math:`S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}\left[2a+(n-1)d\right] \quad
       \text{OR} \quad S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(a+t_n)`
     - **Geometric**

       Sequence: :math:`t_n = a \cdot r^{n-1}`

       Series: :math:`S_n = \dfrac{a(r^n-1)}{r-1}`
