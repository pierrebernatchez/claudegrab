**Write the required formulas:**

Arithmetic Term Formula:

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`t_n = a+(n-1)d`

Arithmetic Series Formula:

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}[2a+(n-1)d]` OR :math:`S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(a+t_n)`

Geometric Term Formula:

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`t_n = a\cdot r^{n-1}`

Geometric Series Formula:

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S_n = \dfrac{a(r^n-1)}{r-1}`

**7)** Given :math:`t_8=72` and :math:`t_{14}=54` of an arithmetic
sequence, determine :math:`a` and :math:`d`, write the general term
formula :math:`t_n`, and calculate :math:`t_{21}`.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`72=a+7d` and :math:`54=a+13d`; subtracting gives :math:`18=-6d`, so
:math:`d=-3` and :math:`a=93`.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`t_n = 93+(n-1)(-3)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`t_{21} = 93+(21-1)(-3) = 93-60 = 33`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**8)** Given :math:`t_5=72` and :math:`t_3=8` of a geometric sequence,
determine :math:`a` and :math:`r`, write the general term formula
:math:`t_n`, and calculate :math:`t_8`.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`72=ar^4` and :math:`8=ar^2`; substituting :math:`a=\dfrac{72}{r^4}`
into the second equation gives :math:`8=\dfrac{72}{r^2}`, so
:math:`r^2=9` and :math:`r=3`. Then :math:`a=\dfrac{72}{81}=\dfrac{8}{9}`.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`t_n = \dfrac{8}{9}(3)^{n-1}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`t_8 = \dfrac{8}{9}(3)^{8-1} = 1944`

Section 2: Series
================================================================================

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**10)** Find the sum of the first 28 terms of the arithmetic series that
starts as: :math:`-6-1+4+9+\ldots`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`a=-6,\ d=5,\ n=28`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S_{28} = \dfrac{28}{2}[2(-6)+(28-1)(5)] = 14[123] = 1722`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**11)** Find the sum of the first 11 terms of the series that begins
with: :math:`1024+512+256+\ldots`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`a=1024,\ r=\dfrac{1}{2},\ n=11`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S_{11} = \dfrac{1024\left[\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{11}-1\right]}
{\frac{1}{2}-1} = 1024\left(\dfrac{-2047}{2048}\right)\div\left(
-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = 2047`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**12)** Find :math:`S_{14}` given :math:`t_n = 5+\dfrac{3}{2}n`

.. rst-class:: solution

arithmetic; :math:`t_1 = 5+\dfrac{3}{2}(1)=\dfrac{13}{2}`,
:math:`t_{14} = 5+\dfrac{3}{2}(14)=26`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S_{14} = \dfrac{14}{2}\left(\dfrac{13}{2}+26\right) =
7\left(\dfrac{65}{2}\right) = \dfrac{455}{2}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**13)** Find :math:`S_7` given :math:`t_n = 5(2^{n-1})`.

.. rst-class:: solution

geometric; :math:`a=5,\ r=2,\ n=7`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S_7 = \dfrac{5(2^7-1)}{2-1} = 5(127) = 635`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**14)** What is the sum of the arithmetic series :math:`2\sqrt{3}+5
\sqrt{3}+8\sqrt{3}+\cdots+83\sqrt{3}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`a=2\sqrt{3},\ d=3\sqrt{3},\ t_n=83\sqrt{3}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`83\sqrt{3}=2\sqrt{3}+(n-1)(3\sqrt{3}) \Rightarrow 81\sqrt{3}=
(n-1)(3\sqrt{3}) \Rightarrow n=28`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S_{28} = \dfrac{28}{2}\left(2\sqrt{3}+83\sqrt{3}\right) =
14\left(85\sqrt{3}\right) = 1190\sqrt{3}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**15)** Determine the sum of the geometric series :math:`5+10+20+\cdots
+2560`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`a=5,\ r=2,\ t_n=2560`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`2560=5(2)^{n-1} \Rightarrow 512=2^{n-1} \Rightarrow 2^9=2^{n-1}
\Rightarrow n=10`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S_{10} = \dfrac{5(2^{10}-1)}{2-1} = 5(1023) = 5115`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**16)** The 23rd term in an arithmetic sequence is 95. The 31st term is
127. Find the sum of the first 31 terms in the series.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`95=a+22d` and :math:`127=a+30d`; subtracting gives :math:`32=8d`,
so :math:`d=4` and :math:`a=7`.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S_{31} = \dfrac{31}{2}(7+127) = \dfrac{31}{2}(134) = 31(67) = 2077`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**17)** The sum of the first 10 terms in an arithmetic sequence is 145.
The 5th term in the series is 16. Find the sum of the first 20 terms in
the series.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`145=\dfrac{10}{2}[2a+9d] \Rightarrow 29=2a+9d` and :math:`16=a+4d`;
solving gives :math:`a=28,\ d=-3`.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S_{20} = \dfrac{20}{2}[2(28)+(20-1)(-3)] = 10(56-57) = -10`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**18)** The third term of a geometric series is 24 and the fourth term
is 36. Determine the sum of the first 10 terms.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`r=\dfrac{36}{24}=\dfrac{3}{2}`; :math:`24=a\left(\dfrac{3}{2}
\right)^2 \Rightarrow a=\dfrac{96}{9}=\dfrac{32}{3}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S_{10} = \dfrac{\frac{32}{3}\left[\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{10}-1
\right]}{\frac{3}{2}-1} = \dfrac{58\,025}{48}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**19)** A lottery awards prizes such that the grand winner gets $100 000,
and the next winner gets one half what the previous winner did. If the
lottery pays out a total of 11 prizes, how much money must they pay out?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`a=100\,000,\ r=\dfrac{1}{2},\ n=11`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`S_{11} = \dfrac{100\,000\left[\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{11}-1
\right]}{\frac{1}{2}-1} = \$199\,902.34`
