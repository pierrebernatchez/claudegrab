Answer Key
================================================================================

1\) a\) :math:`4, 7, 10, 13` b\) :math:`50, 25, \dfrac{25}{2},
\dfrac{25}{4}` c\) :math:`100, 5000, 250\,000, 12\,500\,000`

2\) a\) :math:`3, \dfrac{3}{2}, \dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{1}{8}` b\) :math:`0.5,
-0.5, 0.5, -0.5`

3\) a\) :math:`t_n=t_{n-1}+6` b\) :math:`t_n=t_{n-1}-3` c\)
:math:`t_n=2\cdot t_{n-1}` d\) :math:`t_n=\dfrac{t_{n-1}}{2}`

4\) a\) :math:`t_n=t_{n-1}+30` b\) :math:`t_n=-2\cdot t_{n-1}`

5\) :math:`t_n=t_{n-1}+4(n-1)`

6\) a\) :math:`1, 7, 58, 3376` b\) :math:`\dfrac{1}{2}, 4, 18, 74`

7\) :math:`t_n=t_{n-1}+2n-1`
