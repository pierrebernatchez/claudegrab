**1)** Solve by factoring

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`x^2+8x+12=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`(x+6)(x+2)=0`; :math:`x=-6` or :math:`x=-2`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`h^2+9h+18=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`(h+6)(h+3)=0`; :math:`h=-6` or :math:`h=-3`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) :math:`m^2+3m=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`m(m+3)=0`; :math:`m=0` or :math:`m=-3`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

d\) :math:`w^2-18w+56=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`(w-14)(w-4)=0`; :math:`w=14` or :math:`w=4`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

e\) :math:`x^2-2x=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x(x-2)=0`; :math:`x=0` or :math:`x=2`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

f\) :math:`c^2-17c+30=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`(c-15)(c-2)=0`; :math:`c=15` or :math:`c=2`

**2)** Solve

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`3x^2+28x+9=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

Product: 27, sum: 28, factors 27 and 1: :math:`(3x^2+27x)+(x+9)=0`
:math:`\Rightarrow 3x(x+9)+1(x+9)=0 \Rightarrow (x+9)(3x+1)=0`; :math:`x=-9`
or :math:`x=-\dfrac{1}{3}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`4k^2+19k+15=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

Product: 60, sum: 19, factors 4 and 15:
:math:`(4k^2+4k)+(15k+15)=0 \Rightarrow 4k(k+1)+15(k+1)=0 \Rightarrow
(k+1)(4k+15)=0`; :math:`k=-1` or :math:`k=-\dfrac{15}{4}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

d\) :math:`16b^2-1=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

Difference of squares: :math:`(4b-1)(4b+1)=0`; :math:`b=\dfrac{1}{4}` or
:math:`b=-\dfrac{1}{4}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

f\) :math:`4x^2-12x+9=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

Perfect square trinomial: :math:`(2x-3)^2=0`; :math:`x=\dfrac{3}{2}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**3)** Solve each quadratic equation by factoring

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`x^2+2x-3=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

Product: -3, sum: 2, factors 3 and -1: :math:`(x+3)(x-1)=0`; :math:`x=-3`
or :math:`x=1`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`x^2+3x-10=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

Product: -10, sum: 3, factors 5 and -2: :math:`(x+5)(x-2)=0`; :math:`x=-5`
or :math:`x=2`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) :math:`4x^2-36=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

Difference of squares: :math:`(2x-6)(2x+6)=0`; :math:`x=3` or :math:`x=-3`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

d\) :math:`6x^2-14x+8=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

Common factor 2: :math:`2(3x^2-7x+4)=0 \Rightarrow 3x^2-7x+4=0`. Product:
12, sum: -7, factors -3 and -4:
:math:`(3x^2-3x)+(-4x+4)=0 \Rightarrow 3x(x-1)-4(x-1)=0 \Rightarrow
(3x-4)(x-1)=0`; :math:`x=\dfrac{4}{3}` or :math:`x=1`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

e\) :math:`15x^2-8x+1=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

Product: 15, sum: -8, factors -5 and -3:
:math:`(15x^2-5x)+(-3x+1)=0 \Rightarrow 5x(3x-1)-1(3x-1)=0 \Rightarrow
(3x-1)(5x-1)=0`; :math:`x=\dfrac{1}{3}` or :math:`x=\dfrac{1}{5}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

f\) :math:`6x^2+19x+10=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

Product: 60, sum: 19, factors 15 and 4:
:math:`(6x^2+15x)+(4x+10)=0 \Rightarrow 3x(2x+5)+2(2x+5)=0 \Rightarrow
(2x+5)(3x+2)=0`; :math:`x=-\dfrac{5}{2}` or :math:`x=-\dfrac{2}{3}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**4)** Solve by factoring

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) :math:`-x^2-10x-16=0`

.. rst-class:: solution

Common factor -1: :math:`-1(x^2+10x+16)=0 \Rightarrow x^2+10x+16=0
\Rightarrow (x+2)(x+8)=0`; :math:`x=-2` or :math:`x=-8`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) :math:`6d^2+15d=-9`

.. rst-class:: solution

Rearrange: :math:`6d^2+15d+9=0`. Common factor 3:
:math:`3(2d^2+5d+3)=0 \Rightarrow 2d^2+5d+3=0`. Product: 6, sum: 5,
factors 2 and 3:
:math:`(2d^2+2d)+(3d+3)=0 \Rightarrow 2d(d+1)+3(d+1)=0 \Rightarrow
(d+1)(2d+3)=0`; :math:`d=-1` or :math:`d=-\dfrac{3}{2}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**5)** A rectangle has dimensions :math:`x+10` and :math:`2x-3`.
Determine the value of :math:`x` that gives an area of 54 cm\ :sup:`2`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`(x+10)(2x-3)=54 \Rightarrow 2x^2+17x-30=54 \Rightarrow
2x^2+17x-84=0`. Product: -168, sum: 17, factors 24 and -7:
:math:`(2x^2+24x)+(-7x-84)=0 \Rightarrow 2x(x+12)-7(x+12)=0 \Rightarrow
(x+12)(2x-7)=0`; :math:`x=-12` or :math:`x=\dfrac{7}{2}`

.. rst-class:: solution

A negative value for :math:`x` is rejected since it's a dimension, so
:math:`x=3.5` cm.
