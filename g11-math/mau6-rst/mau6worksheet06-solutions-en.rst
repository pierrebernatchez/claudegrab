**3)** The height, h, in meters, of the tide in a given location on a
given day at t hours after midnight can be modeled using the sinusoidal
function :math:`h(t) = 5\sin[30(t-5)]+7`.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Find the max and min values for the depth of water.

.. rst-class:: solution

From the equation, :math:`c=7` and :math:`a=5`, so the function has a
midline value of 7 and an amplitude of 5. The maximum height is 12 m
and the minimum height is 2 m.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) What time is high tide? What time is low tide?

.. rst-class:: solution

From the equation, :math:`k=30` and :math:`d=5`, so the period is 12 h
and the phase shift is 5 h right. The first midline value occurs at
5:00 a.m. The first maximum occurs one-quarter period, or 3 h after
this, at 8:00 a.m. The previous minimum is 3 h prior to 5:00 a.m., at
2:00 a.m. Because of the 12-h period, there will also be a maximum at
8:00 p.m. and a minimum at 2:00 p.m.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) What is the depth of the water at 9:00 am?

.. rst-class:: solution

11.3 m

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

d\) Find all the times during a 24-h period when the depth of the water
is 3 meters.

.. rst-class:: solution

The solution gives a time of approximately 3:14 a.m. This time is 1 h
14 min after the first minimum so the depth should also occur 1 h 14
min before 2:00 a.m, at 12:46 a.m. Because of the 12-h period, the
depth will also occur at 12:46 p.m. and 3:14 p.m.

**4)** The population, P, of a lakeside town with a large number of
seasonal residents can be modeled using the function :math:`P(t) =
5000\sin[30(t-7)]+8000`, where t is the number of months after New
Year's Day.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Find the max and min values for the population over a whole year.

.. rst-class:: solution

From the equation, :math:`c=8000` and :math:`a=5000`, so the function
has a midline value of 8000 and an amplitude of 5000. The maximum
population is 13 000 and the minimum population is 3000.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) When is the population a maximum? When is it a minimum?

.. rst-class:: solution

From the equation, :math:`k=30` and :math:`d=7`, so the period is 12
months and the phase shift is 7 months right. The initial midline value
occurs at :math:`t=7`. The maximum occurs 3 months later at
:math:`t=10` (October) and the minimum 3 months earlier at :math:`t=4`
(April).

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) What is the population on September 30th?

.. rst-class:: solution

12 330

**5)** The population of prey in a predator-prey relation is shown. Time
is in years since 1985.

.. image:: ../images/mau6worksheet06-gpimage03.png
   :scale: 100

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Determine the max and min values of the population, to the nearest
50. Use these to find the amplitude.

.. rst-class:: solution

From the graph the maximum population is approximately 850 animals and
the minimum population is approximately 250 animals. The amplitude is
approximately 300 animals, so :math:`a=300`.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) Determine the vertical shift, c.

.. rst-class:: solution

The vertical shift is the maximum value minus the amplitude, so
:math:`c=550`.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) Determine the phase shift, d.

.. rst-class:: solution

The midline intersects the graph at :math:`t=0` so no horizontal shift
is necessary, so :math:`d=0`.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

d\) Determine the period. Use the period to determine the value of k.

.. rst-class:: solution

The pattern repeats every 6 years, so the period is 6 years.
:math:`k=60`.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

e\) Model the population versus time with a sinusoidal function.

.. rst-class:: solution

A sine function that models the population of prey, N, with respect to
time, t, is :math:`N = 300\sin 60t+550`.
