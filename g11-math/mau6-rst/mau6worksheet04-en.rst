Answer Key
================================================================================

1\) a) :math:`y=5\sin[3(x+30\degree)]-2` b) :math:`y=5\cos 3x-2`

2\) a) :math:`y=\frac{1}{2}\sin[\frac{1}{2}(x+180\degree)]+1` b)
:math:`y=\frac{1}{2}\cos\frac{1}{2}x+1`

3\) :math:`y=\cos[4(x-82.5\degree)]+2`

4\) :math:`y=3\sin(9000x)+8` OR :math:`y=3\cos[9000(x-0.01)]+8`

5\) :math:`y=2\sin[6(x-15\degree)]+1`

6\) :math:`y=4\cos 2x` and :math:`y=4\sin[2(x+45\degree)]`
