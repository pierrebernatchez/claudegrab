Section 1: Remembering the Unit Circle
================================================================================

The circle being used has radius :math:`r`. The radius and the
coordinates of a point on the circle :math:`(x,y)` are related to the
primary trig ratios.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\sin\theta = \dfrac{y}{r}` :math:`\quad\quad\quad \cos\theta =
\dfrac{x}{r} \quad\quad\quad \tan\theta = \dfrac{y}{x}`

A UNIT CIRCLE has a radius of 1.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\sin\theta = y \quad\quad\quad \cos\theta = x \quad\quad\quad
\tan\theta = \dfrac{y}{x}`

Summary of findings for trig ratios using the unit circle:

The sine function: graphs the relationship between the angle and the

.. rst-class:: solution

**VERTICAL**

displacement from the x-axis.

The cosine function: graphs the relationship between the angle and the

.. rst-class:: solution

**HORIZONTAL**

displacement from the y-axis.

Section 2: Modeling with Graphs
================================================================================

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**Example 1:** You are in a car of a Ferris wheel. The wheel has a
radius of 8m and turns counterclockwise. Let the origin be at the center
of the wheel. Begin your sketch when the radius from the center of the
wheel to your car is along the positive x-axis.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Sketch the graph of vertical displacement versus the angle of
rotation for 1 complete rotation.

.. image:: ../images/mau6lesson05-gpimage01.png
   :scale: 100

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 8\sin x`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) Sketch the graph of horizontal displacement versus the angle of
rotation for 1 complete rotation starting along the positive x-axis.

.. image:: ../images/mau6lesson05-gpimage02.png
   :scale: 100

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 8\cos x`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) Sketch the graph of horizontal displacement versus the angle of
rotation for 1 complete rotation if your car starts at the bottom of the
Ferris Wheel.

.. image:: ../images/mau6lesson05-gpimage03.png
   :scale: 100

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 8\sin x` AND :math:`y = 8\cos(x-90)`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**Example 2:** A carousel rotates at a constant speed. It has a diameter
of 15m. A horse that is directly in line with the center, horizontally,
rotates around 3 full times. Create a graph that models the horizontal
distance from the center as the horse rotates around.

.. rst-class:: solution

Note: :math:`radius = \dfrac{15}{2} = 7.5`

.. image:: ../images/mau6lesson05-gpimage04.png
   :scale: 100

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 7.5\cos x`

Section 3: Modeling with Equations
================================================================================

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**Example 3:** A group of students is tracking a friend, John, who is
riding a Ferris wheel. They know that John reaches the maximum height of
11m at 10 seconds, and then reaches the minimum height of 1m at 55
seconds.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Develop an equation of a sine and cosine function that models John's
height above the ground.

.. image:: ../images/mau6lesson05-gpimage05.png
   :scale: 100

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`a = \dfrac{11-1}{2} = 5`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`k = \dfrac{360}{100-10} = \dfrac{360}{90} = 4`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`c = 11-5 = 6`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`d_{cos} = 10`, :math:`d_{sin} = 10-\dfrac{90}{4} = -12.5`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 5\cos[4(x-10)]+6` AND :math:`y = 5\sin[4(x+12.5)]+6`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) What is John's height above the ground after 78 seconds?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 5\cos[4(78-10)]+6`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 5\cos(272\degree)+6`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 6.2\text{m}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**Example 4:** Don Quixote, a fictional character in a Spanish novel,
attacked windmills because he thought they were giants. At one point, he
got snagged by one of the blades and was hoisted into the air. The
center of the windmill is 10 meters off the ground and each blade is 7
meters long. The blade picked him up when it was at its lowest point.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Graph Don's height above the ground during one full rotation around
the windmill.

.. image:: ../images/mau6lesson05-gpimage06.png
   :scale: 100

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) Determine an equation for a sine and cosine function that represents
his height above the ground in relation to the angle of rotation.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`a = \dfrac{17-3}{2} = 7`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`k = \dfrac{360}{360} = 1`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`c = 17-7 = 10`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`d_{cos} = 180`, :math:`d_{sin} = 90`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 7\cos(x-180)+10` AND :math:`y = 7\sin(x-90)+10`
