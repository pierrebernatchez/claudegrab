Answer Key
================================================================================

1\) a) :math:`y=-67.5\cos x` b) amplitude 67.5, period 360°

2\) a) :math:`y=67.5\sin x` b) amplitude 67.5, period 360°

3\) a) :math:`y=12\sin x` b) :math:`y=12\cos x` c) 6 full cycles (six
12-h periods in 72 h)

4\) a) :math:`y=10\sin(x-120\degree)+14` b)
:math:`y=10\cos(x-210\degree)+14` c)
:math:`y=10\sin(x-150\degree)+14` and :math:`y=10\cos(x-240\degree)+14`

5\) a) :math:`y=10\sin(x-120\degree)+16` b)
:math:`y=10\cos(x-210\degree)+16`
