   **3)** A periodic function :math:`f(x)` has a period of 8. The values of
   :math:`f(1), f(5),` and :math:`f(7)` are -3, 2, and 8, respectively.
   Predict the value of each of the following. If a prediction is not
   possible, explain why not.

   .. list-table::
      :widths: 50 50
      :width: 100%

      * - .. rst-class:: keepwithnext

          a\) :math:`f(9)`

          .. rst-class:: solution

          :math:`f(9) = f(9-8) = f(1) = -3`
        - .. rst-class:: keepwithnext

          b\) :math:`f(29)`

          .. rst-class:: solution

          :math:`f(29) = f[29-3(8)] = f(29-24) = f(5) = 2`

      * - .. rst-class:: keepwithnext

          c\) :math:`f(63)`

          .. rst-class:: solution

          :math:`f(63) = f[63-7(8)] = f(63-56) = f(7) = 8`
        - .. rst-class:: keepwithnext

          d\) :math:`f(40)`

          .. rst-class:: solution

          No prediction possible. :math:`x = 40-8n`; there are no integer
          values of :math:`n` that make :math:`x = 1, 5,` or :math:`7`.

**4)** The people mover at an airport shuttles between the main terminal
and a satellite terminal 300 meters away. A one-way trip, moving at a
constant speed, takes 1 minute, and the car remains at each terminal for
30 seconds before leaving.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Sketch a graph to represent the distance of the car from the main
terminal with respect to time. Include four complete cycles.

.. image:: ../images/mau6worksheet01-gpimage05.png
   :scale: 100

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) What is the period of the motion?

.. rst-class:: solution

Period :math:`= 4-1 = 3` minutes

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) What is the amplitude of the motion?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\text{amplitude} = \dfrac{\text{max}-\text{min}}{2} =
\dfrac{300-0}{2} = 150` meters

**5)** While visiting the east coast of Canada, Ranouf notices that the
water level at a town dock changes during the day as the tides come in
and go out. Markings on one of the piles supporting the dock show a high
tide of 3.3 meters at 6:30 a.m., a low tide of 0.7 meters at 12:40 p.m.,
and a high tide again at 6:50 p.m.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 100 100
   :width: 100%

   * - .. rst-class:: keepwithnext

       a\) Estimate the period of the fluctuation of the water level at
       the town dock.

       .. rst-class:: solution

       Period :math:`=` difference between 6:30 a.m. and 6:50 p.m.
       :math:`= 12` hours and 20 minutes
     - .. rst-class:: keepwithnext

       b\) Estimate the amplitude of the pattern.

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\text{amplitude} = \dfrac{3.3-0.7}{2} = 1.3` meters

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) Predict when the next low tide will occur.

.. rst-class:: solution

6 hours and 10 minutes between high tide and low tide; therefore the
next low tide is 6 hours and 10 minutes after 6:50 p.m. :math:`= 1:00`
a.m.
