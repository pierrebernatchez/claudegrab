Section 1: Exponential Growth and Decay
================================================================================

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**1)** The population of a pod of Southern right whales has doubled in
size recently over a 9 year period. They have a current population of 38
individuals. What might the population be after 25 more years?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 38(2)^{\frac{25}{9}}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 260.6`; the population will be approximately 261 whales

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**2)** A bacteria culture doubles every 15 minutes. There were 20
individuals initially.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**a)** How many bacteria will be present after 2 hours?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 20(2)^{\frac{120}{15}} = 20(2)^8`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 5120`; there will be 5120 bacteria

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**b)** How long will it take to grow a population of 163 840?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`163\,840 = 20(2)^x \Rightarrow 8192 = 2^x \Rightarrow x =
\dfrac{\log 8192}{\log 2} = 13`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`13 = \dfrac{t}{15} \Rightarrow t = 195`; it will take 195 minutes

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**3)** A weight loss program aims for its clients to lose 5% of their
weight each week. If Helene currently weighs 280 pounds, what can she
expect to weigh at the end of the 12-week program?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 280(0.95)^{12}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 151.3`; Helene can expect to weigh 151.3 lbs

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**4)** The population of a developing city increases by 8% per year. In
2005, 125 000 people lived in the city.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**a)** What is the population expected in 2020?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 125\,000(1.08)^{15}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 396\,521.1`; the population is expected to be approximately
396 521

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**b)** In what year should the population reach half a million?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`500\,000 = 125\,000(1.08)^x \Rightarrow 4 = 1.08^x \Rightarrow x =
\dfrac{\log 4}{\log 1.08} = 18.0`

.. rst-class:: solution

the population will reach a half million by year 2005 + 18 = 2023

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**5)** The half-life of Carbon-14 is approximately 6000 years. A fossil
of Carbon-14 weighed 100 grams. How much would it weigh after 18 000
years?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 100\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{18000}{6000}} =
100\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^3`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 12.5`; it will weigh 12.5 grams

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**6)** A radioactive isotope decays rapidly with a half-life of 4
minutes. For a 12 minute test, technicians need at least 6 grams of the
isotope to remain at the end of the test. How much must they start with?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`6 = a\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{12}{4}} = a\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^3`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`a = \dfrac{6}{\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3} = 48`; they must start
with 48 grams

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**7)** A nuclear power plant has a stockpile of 100 tonnes of radioactive
waste. The radioactive waste has a half-life of 15 years. How long will
it take for there to be only 1 tonne remaining?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`1 = 100\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x \Rightarrow 0.01 =
\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x \Rightarrow x = \dfrac{\log 0.01}{\log 0.5} =
6.64`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`6.64 = \dfrac{t}{15} \Rightarrow t = 99.6` years; it will take
nearly 100 years for there to be only 1 tonne remaining

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**8)** If Canada were to stop accepting immigrants the population would
begin to decrease by 0.5% per year. Canada's population is currently
34 482 779. If it stopped accepting new immigrants today, what would the
population be in 50 years?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 34\,482\,779(0.995)^{50}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 26\,838\,379.9`; in 50 years the population will be
approximately 26 838 380 people

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**9)** The amount of sunlight a diver can see is halved for every 11
meters she dives. What percentage of light remains when the diver is 60
meters below the surface? (Hint: at the surface is 100% of the light)

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 100\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{60}{11}}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 2.28`; 2.28% of light remains 60 m below the surface

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**10)** Lead-210 decays very slowly with a half-life of 180 years. After
1000 years, how much of a 40 kg sample remains?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 40\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{1000}{180}}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 0.85`; approximately 0.85 kg remains after 1000 years

Section 2: Interest
================================================================================

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**11)** A pension increases every year by 6%. If Opa has $24 300 in his
pension right now, how much will his pension be worth in 10 years?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A = P(1+i)^n = 24\,300(1.06)^{10}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A = 43\,517.60`; his pension will be worth $43 517.60

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**12)** An investor invests $1000 into a mutual fund for 4 years at a
growth rate of 2.5% per year. How much is the investment worth after 4
years?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A = 1000(1.025)^4`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A = 1103.81`; the investment will be worth $1103.81

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**13)** You have found an investment at the bank that pays 2.4% per year
for 10 years. How much should you invest now so that you have $2000
after the 10 years?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`2000 = P(1.024)^{10} \Rightarrow P = \dfrac{2000}{(1.024)^{10}}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`P = 1577.72`; you should invest $1577.72

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**14)** Jeremiah's grandparents placed $3000 into an account for him when
he was born. The investment is to be paid out when he turns 25. The
account pays an annual interest rate of 8%, compounded semi-annually. How
much will be in the account on Jeremiah's 25th birthday?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`P = 3000`, :math:`i = \dfrac{0.08}{2} = 0.04`,
:math:`n = 25 \times 2 = 50`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A = 3000(1.04)^{50} = 21\,320.05`; it will be worth $21 320.05
