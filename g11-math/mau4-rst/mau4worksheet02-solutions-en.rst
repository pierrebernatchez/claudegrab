**1)** During medical treatment, the number of bacterial cells in a
patient decreases by a factor of :math:`\dfrac{1}{2}` every day. A
patient has 1 000 000 bacterial cells on Monday, the first day of
treatment.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) How many bacterial cells will remain on Thursday?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A(t) = 1\,000\,000\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^t`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A(3) = 1\,000\,000\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^3`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A(3) = 125\,000`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How many bacterial cells will remain on Sunday?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A(6) = 1\,000\,000\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^6`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A(6) = 15\,625`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) On what day will the number of remaining bacterial cells be 1950?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`1950 = 1\,000\,000\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^t`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`0.00195 = \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^t`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`t = \log_{1/2}(0.00195)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`t \approx 9` days (Wednesday, the following week)

**3)** Shylo is very excited about her brand new car! Although she paid
\$20 000 for the car, its resale value will depreciate (decrease) by 30%
of its current value every year.

.. rst-class:: solution

decay factor of :math:`1 - 0.3 = 0.7`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Write an equation relating the car's depreciated value, :math:`v`, in
dollars, to the time, :math:`t`, in years since her purchase.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`v(t) = 20\,000(0.7)^t`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How much will Shylo's car be worth in...

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :width: 100%

   * - i\) 1 year?

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`v(1) = 20\,000(0.7)^1`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= 14\,000` (\$14 000)
     - ii\) 2 years?

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`v(2) = 20\,000(0.7)^2`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`= 9800` (\$9800)

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) Graph the depreciation function.

.. image:: ../images/mau4worksheet02-gpimage02.png
   :scale: 95

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

d\) How long will it take for Shylo's car to depreciate to 10% of its
original price?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`2000 = 20\,000(0.7)^t`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`0.1 = (0.7)^t`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`t = \log_{0.7}(0.1)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`t \approx 6.46` years

**4)** An isotope of a radioactive substance has a half-life of 23 days.
Suppose that you start with an 800-mg sample of the material.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Find an equation that models this data

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A(t) = 800\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{t/23}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) Use the equation to determine the amount of substance left after 100
days

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A(100) = 800\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{100/23} \approx 39.29` mg
