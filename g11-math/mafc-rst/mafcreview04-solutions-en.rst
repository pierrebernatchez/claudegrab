Section 1: Exponential Growth
================================================================================

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**1)** An insect colony has an initial population of 15. The number of
insects quadruples every day.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Determine a function that models this exponential growth.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`a=15,\ b=4 \implies P(n) = 15(4)^n`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How many insects will be present in 1 week?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`P(7) = 15(4)^7 = 245\,760`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**2)** If the population of an ant colony is 213 and it doubles every week,

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) What will the population be in 4 weeks?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 213(2)^4 = 3408`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How long will it take the population to reach 109 056 ants?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`109\,056 = 213(2)^x \implies 512 = 2^x \implies 2^9 = 2^x \implies x=9`
weeks

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**3)** The population of a town in the Northwest Territories starts off at
20,000 and grows by 13% each year. Find the population after 10 years.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 20\,000(1.13)^{10} = 67\,891.35`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**4)** There are 50 bacteria present initially in a culture. In 3 min., the
count is 204800. What is the doubling period?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`204\,800 = 50(2)^{3/t} \implies 4096 = 2^{3/t} \implies 2^{12} =
2^{3/t}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`12 = \dfrac{3}{t} \implies t = \dfrac{1}{4}` minute, i.e. the
doubling period is 15 seconds.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**5)** A bacteria culture starts with a population of 12 000 and doubles
every four hours.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) How many bacteria are present after 12 hours?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 12\,000(2)^{12/4} = 12\,000(2)^3 = 96\,000`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How many bacteria are present after 1 day?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 12\,000(2)^{24/4} = 12\,000(2)^6 = 768\,000`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) How long will it take for the population of the bacteria to reach
49 152 000?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`49\,152\,000 = 12\,000(2)^{t/4} \implies 4096 = 2^{t/4} \implies
2^{12} = 2^{t/4} \implies t = 48` hours, i.e. 2 days.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**6)** A bacteria culture doubles every 15 minutes. There were 20
individuals initially.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) How many bacteria will be present after 3 hours (180 minutes)?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 20(2)^{180/15} = 20(2)^{12} = 81\,920`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How long will it take to grow a population of 163 840?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`163\,840 = 20(2)^{t/15} \implies 8192 = 2^{t/15} \implies 2^{13} =
2^{t/15} \implies t = 195` minutes

Section 2: Exponential Decay
================================================================================

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**7)** In 1976, a research hospital bought half of a gram of radium for
cancer research. Assuming the hospital still exists, how much of this
radium will the hospital have in the year 6836, if the half-life of the
radium is 1620 years?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 0.5\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{4860/1620} = 0.5\left(\dfrac{1}{2}
\right)^3 = 0.0625` g

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**8)** Polonium-210 is a radioactive isotope that has a half-life of 20
days. Suppose you start with a 40-mg sample.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Write an equation that relates the amount of polonium-210 remaining and
time.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`f(t) = 40\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{t/20}`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How much polonium-210 will remain after 10 weeks (70 days)?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`f(70) = 40\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{70/20} = 3.54` mg

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) How long will it take for the amount of polonium-210 to decay to 8% of
its initial mass?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`0.08 = \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{t/20} \implies \dfrac{\log 0.08}
{\log 0.5} = \dfrac{t}{20}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`t = 20(3.643856) \approx 72.88`, i.e. about 73 days

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**9)** A cup of coffee contains approximately 96 mg of caffeine. When you
drink the coffee, the caffeine is absorbed into the bloodstream and is
eventually metabolized by the body. Every 5 hours the amount of caffeine
present in the body is reduced by one-half. How many hours does it take for
the amount of caffeine to be reduced to 12 mg?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`12 = 96\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{t/5} \implies \dfrac{1}{8} =
\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{t/5} \implies \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^3 =
\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{t/5} \implies t = 15` hours

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**10)** Daniel is very excited about his new motorcycle. Although the
motorcycle costs $13 500, its resale value will depreciate by 20% of its
current value every year.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) How much will the motorcycle be worth in 6 years?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y = 13\,500(1-0.2)^6 = 13\,500(0.8)^6 = 3538.94`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How long will it take for Daniel's motorcycle to depreciate to 50% of
its original cost?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`0.5(13\,500) = 13\,500(0.8)^x \implies 0.5 = 0.8^x \implies x =
\dfrac{\log 0.5}{\log 0.8} = 3.1` years

Section 3: Interest
================================================================================

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**11)** An investment opportunity is found that makes 7% per year compounded
annually. How much should you invest now if you need $13,450 at the end of
9 years?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`13\,450 = P(1.07)^9 \implies P = \dfrac{13\,450}{(1.07)^9} =
7315.91`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**12)** Jacqueline deposits an inheritance of $1500 into an account that
earns interest of 3.5% per year, compounded annually.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) How much is in the account after 8 years?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A = 1500(1.035)^8 = 1975.21`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How long will it take for the money to double (round to the nearest
year)?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`2(1500) = 1500(1.035)^n \implies 2 = (1.035)^n \implies n =
\dfrac{\log 2}{\log 1.035} = 20.1488`, i.e. about 20 years

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**13)** Five years ago, Denise deposited an amount into an account that
pays 7.5% per year, compounded annually. Today the account balance is
$4200.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) What was the amount of Denise's initial deposit?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`4200 = P(1.075)^5 \implies P = \dfrac{4200}{(1.075)^5} = 2925.55`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How much was in the account 2 years ago?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`4200 = P(1.075)^2 \implies P = \dfrac{4200}{(1.075)^2} = 3634.40`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) How much will be in the account 2 years from now?

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`A = 4200(1.075)^2 = 4853.63`
