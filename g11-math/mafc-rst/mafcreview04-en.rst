Section 1: Exponential Growth
================================================================================

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**1)** An insect colony has an initial population of 15. The number of
insects quadruples every day.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Determine a function that models this exponential growth.

|nbsp|

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How many insects will be present in 1 week?

|nbsp|

**2)** If the population of an ant colony is 213 and it doubles every week,

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) What will the population be in 4 weeks?

|nbsp|

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How long will it take the population to reach 109 056 ants?

|nbsp|

Section 2: Exponential Decay
================================================================================

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**7)** In 1976, a research hospital bought half of a gram of radium for
cancer research. Assuming the hospital still exists, how much of this
radium will the hospital have in the year 6836, if the half-life of the
radium is 1620 years?

|nbsp|

|nbsp|

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**8)** Polonium-210 is a radioactive isotope that has a half-life of 20
days. Suppose you start with a 40-mg sample.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Write an equation that relates the amount of polonium-210 remaining and
time.

|nbsp|

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How much polonium-210 will remain after 10 weeks?

|nbsp|

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

c\) How long will it take for the amount of polonium-210 to decay to 8% of
its initial mass?

|nbsp|

|nbsp|

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**9)** A cup of coffee contains approximately 96 mg of caffeine. When you
drink the coffee, the caffeine is absorbed into the bloodstream and is
eventually metabolized by the body. Every 5 hours the amount of caffeine
present in the body is reduced by one-half. How many hours does it take for
the amount of caffeine to be reduced to 12 mg?

|nbsp|

|nbsp|

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**10)** Daniel is very excited about his new motorcycle. Although the
motorcycle costs $13 500, its resale value will depreciate by 20% of its
current value every year.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) How much will the motorcycle be worth in 6 years?

|nbsp|

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

b\) How long will it take for Daniel's motorcycle to depreciate to 50% of
its original cost?

|nbsp|
