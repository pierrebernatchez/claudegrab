The reciprocal trigonometric ratios are reciprocals of the primary
trigonometric ratios, and are defined as 1 divided by each of the primary
trigonometric ratios:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :width: 100%

   * - Primary Trig Ratios
     - Reciprocal Trig Ratios
   * - :math:`\sin\theta = \dfrac{opposite}{hypotenuse}`
     - :math:`cosecant = \dfrac{1}{\sin\theta} = \dfrac{hypotenuse}{opposite}`
   * - :math:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{adjacent}{hypotenuse}`
     - :math:`secant = \dfrac{1}{\cos\theta} = \dfrac{hypotenuse}{adjacent}`
   * - :math:`\tan\theta = \dfrac{opposite}{adjacent}`
     - :math:`cotangent = \dfrac{1}{\tan\theta} = \dfrac{adjacent}{opposite}`
