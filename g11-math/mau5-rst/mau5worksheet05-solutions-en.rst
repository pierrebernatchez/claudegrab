**1)** The shadow of a tree that is 12 meters tall measures 9 meters in
length. Determine the angle of elevation of the sun.

.. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet05-gpimage01.png
   :scale: 90

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\tan\theta = \dfrac{12}{9}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{12}{9}\right)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\theta \approx 53.1\degree`

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**2)** Yolanda flies her ultra-light airplane due east for 100 km. She
turns right through an angle of 130°, and flies a second leg. Then, she
turns right 110° and returns to her starting point.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

a\) Represent the flight path using an appropriate diagram, labeling all
information.

.. rst-class:: solution

.. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet05-gpimage02.png
   :scale: 95

b\) Determine the total length of the flight, to the nearest km.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\dfrac{x}{\sin(60\degree)} = \dfrac{100}{\sin(70\degree)}`;
:math:`\dfrac{y}{\sin(50\degree)} = \dfrac{100}{\sin(70\degree)}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x = \dfrac{100\sin(60\degree)}{\sin(70\degree)} \approx 92.2`
km; :math:`y = \dfrac{100\sin(50\degree)}{\sin(70\degree)} \approx 81.5`
km

.. rst-class:: solution

Length of flight :math:`\approx 92.2 + 81.5 + 100 \approx 273.7` km
:math:`\approx 274` km

**3)** A radio antenna is stabilized by two guy wires. One guy wire is
100 m in length and is attached to the top of the antenna. The wire
makes an angle of 60° with the ground. One end of the second guy wire is
attached to the ground at the same point as the first guy wire. The
other end is attached to the antenna such that the wire makes an angle
of 45° with the ground. Determine an exact expression for the distance
between the points where the two guy wires are attached to the antenna.

.. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet05-gpimage03.png
   :scale: 90

.. rst-class:: solution

.. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet05-gpimage04.png
   :scale: 60

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\sin(60\degree) = \dfrac{y_1}{100}`,
:math:`\cos(60\degree) = \dfrac{x}{100}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y_1 = 100\sin(60\degree) = \dfrac{100\sqrt{3}}{2} = 50\sqrt{3}`;
:math:`x = 100\cos(60\degree) = 50`

.. rst-class:: solution

.. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet05-gpimage05.png
   :scale: 60

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\tan(45\degree) = \dfrac{y_2}{50}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y_2 = 50\tan(45\degree) = 50`

.. rst-class:: solution

The distance between where the wires are attached is
:math:`y_1 - y_2 = 50\sqrt{3} - 50 = 50(\sqrt{3}-1)` m

**4)** A Ferris wheel has a radius of 20 m, with 10 cars spaced around
the circumference at equal distances. If the cars are numbered in
order, how far is it directly from the first car to the fifth car?

.. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet05-gpimage06.png
   :scale: 95

.. rst-class:: solution

The angle between the 1st and 5th cars (4 gaps of
:math:`\dfrac{360\degree}{10} = 36\degree` each) is
:math:`4 \times 36\degree = 144\degree`.

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x^2 = 20^2 + 20^2 - 2(20)(20)\cos(144\degree)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x \approx 38` m

**5)** Determine the value of :math:`x` to the nearest cm.

.. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet05-gpimage07.png
   :scale: 90

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\tan(35\degree) = \dfrac{y}{15}`;
:math:`15\tan(35\degree) = y`; :math:`y \approx 10.5` cm

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\sin(45\degree) = \dfrac{10.5}{x}`;
:math:`x = \dfrac{10.5}{\sin(45\degree)}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x \approx 15` cm

**6)** Determine the measure of :math:`\theta` to the nearest degree.

.. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet05-gpimage08.png
   :scale: 90

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x^2 = 18^2 + 14^2 - 2(18)(14)\cos(95\degree)`;
:math:`x \approx 23.75`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`18^2 + 15^2 = y^2`; :math:`y \approx 23.43`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`14^2 + 15^2 = z^2`; :math:`z \approx 20.52`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\cos\theta = \dfrac{x^2 - y^2 - z^2}{-2yz} =
\dfrac{23.75^2 - 23.43^2 - 20.52^2}{-2(23.43)(20.52)} \approx
\dfrac{-405.9729}{-961.5672}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\theta \approx 65\degree`

**7)** Bert wants to calculate the height of a tree on the opposite
bank of a river. To do this, he lays out a baseline 80 m long and
measures the angles as shown. The angle of elevation from A to the top
of the tree is 28°. Calculate the height of the tree to the nearest
meter.

.. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet05-gpimage09.png
   :scale: 95

.. rst-class:: solution

In triangle ABC, :math:`\angle ACB = 180\degree - 85\degree - 30\degree
= 65\degree`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\dfrac{80}{\sin(65\degree)} = \dfrac{AC}{\sin(30\degree)}`;
:math:`AC = \dfrac{80\sin(30\degree)}{\sin(65\degree)} \approx 44.1`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\tan(28\degree) = \dfrac{h}{44.1}`;
:math:`h \approx 44.1\tan(28\degree)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`h \approx 23` m

**8)** The bases on a baseball diamond are 27.4 m apart. The pitcher
pitches, and the batter hits a fly ball straight up 15 m. What is the
maximum angle of elevation of the ball, to the nearest degree, as seen
by the pitcher if he is standing at the center of the diamond?

.. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet05-gpimage10.png
   :scale: 95

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y^2 = 27.4^2 + 27.4^2`; :math:`y \approx 38.75`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x = \dfrac{38.75}{2} \approx 19.4`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\tan\theta = \dfrac{15}{19.4}`;
:math:`\theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{15}{19.4}\right)`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\theta \approx 38\degree`

**9)** Ranjeet parks his car in a lot on the corner of Park Lane and
Main Street. He walks 80 m east to First Avenue, turns 30° to the
left, and follows First Avenue for 100 m to the Metro Building, where
he takes the elevator to his office on the 15\ :sup:`th` floor. Each
floor in the building is 4 m in height. From his office window,
Ranjeet can see his car in the lot. How far is Ranjeet from his car,
in a direct line to the nearest meter?

.. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet05-gpimage11.png
   :scale: 95

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x^2 = 80^2 + 100^2 - 2(80)(100)\cos(150\degree)`;
:math:`x \approx 174` m

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`y^2 = 174^2 + 60^2`; :math:`y \approx 184` m
