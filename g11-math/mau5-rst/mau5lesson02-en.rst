Part 1: Reference Angles
================================================================================

``______________``: Always lies on the positive :math:`x`-axis at 0°.
Meets the terminal arm at the origin.

``______________``: The arm that rotates around the origin counter
clockwise to form a positive angle or clockwise for a negative angle.

``__________`` is measured from the initial arm to the terminal arm.

.. image:: ../images/mau5lesson02-gpimage01.png
   :scale: 70

``______________``: The counter clockwise angle between the initial arm
and the terminal arm of an angle in standard position. It's value is
between 0° and 360°.

``______________________________``: The acute angle between the
terminal arm of an angle in standard position and the closest
:math:`x`-axis when the terminal arm lies in quadrant 2, 3, or 4.

The reference angle helps us determine the exact trig ratios when we
are given obtuse angles.

Part 4: Negative and Co-terminal Angles
================================================================================

Co-terminal angles are angles in standard position that have the
``________________``.

Starting at 30° and rotating 360° counter clockwise will bring you back
to the same terminal arm.

:math:`30\degree + 360\degree = 390\degree`

Therefore, 30° and 390° are co-terminal.
