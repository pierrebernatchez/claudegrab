**1)** In triangle ABC, :math:`a = 13` cm, :math:`b = 21` cm, and
:math:`\angle A = 29\degree`. Draw possible diagrams that match the
given measurements. Then calculate the length of side :math:`c`.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :width: 100%

   * - .. rst-class:: keepwithnext

       **Case 1:**

       .. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet06-gpimage01.png
          :scale: 70

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\dfrac{13}{\sin(29\degree)} = \dfrac{21}{\sin B}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\sin B = \dfrac{21\sin(29\degree)}{13}`;
       :math:`\angle B \approx 51.55\degree`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\angle C = 180\degree - 29\degree - 51.55\degree
       \approx 99.45\degree`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\dfrac{c}{\sin(99.45\degree)} = \dfrac{13}{\sin(29\degree)}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`c \approx 26.45` cm
     - .. rst-class:: keepwithnext

       **Case 2:**

       .. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet06-gpimage02.png
          :scale: 70

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\angle B = 180\degree - 51.55\degree \approx 128.45\degree`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\angle C = 180\degree - 29\degree - 128.45\degree
       \approx 22.55\degree`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`c = \dfrac{13\sin(22.55\degree)}{\sin(29\degree)}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`c \approx 10.26` cm

**3)** In triangle ABC, :math:`a = 2.4` cm, :math:`c = 3.2` cm, and
:math:`\angle A = 28\degree`. Determine two possible measures for
:math:`\angle C` and for the length of side :math:`b`.

   :widths: 50 50

       **Case 1:**

       .. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet06-gpimage05.png

**4)** In triangle DEF, :math:`d = 3` cm, :math:`e = 5` cm, and
:math:`\angle D = 30\degree`. Determine two possible measures for
:math:`\angle E` and for the length of side :math:`f`.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :width: 100%

   * - .. rst-class:: keepwithnext

       **Case 1:**

       .. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet06-gpimage07.png
          :scale: 70

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\dfrac{5}{\sin E} = \dfrac{3}{\sin(30\degree)}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\sin E = \dfrac{5\sin(30\degree)}{3}`;
       :math:`\angle E \approx 56.4\degree`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\angle F = 180\degree - 30\degree - 56.4\degree
       \approx 93.6\degree`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\dfrac{f}{\sin(93.6\degree)} = \dfrac{3}{\sin(30\degree)}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`f \approx 6.0` cm
     - .. rst-class:: keepwithnext

       **Case 2:**

       .. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet06-gpimage08.png
          :scale: 70

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\angle E = 180\degree - 56.4\degree \approx 123.6\degree`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\angle F = 180\degree - 30\degree - 123.6\degree
       \approx 26.4\degree`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`f = \dfrac{3\sin(26.4\degree)}{\sin(30\degree)}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`f \approx 2.7` cm

**5)** Two ships, S and T, are 120 km apart when they pick up a
distress call from a yacht. Ship T estimates that the yacht is 70 km
away and that the angle between the line from T to S and the line
from S to the yacht is 28°. What are two possible distances, to the
nearest tenth of a km, from ship S to the yacht?

.. list-table::
   :widths: 50 50
   :width: 100%

   * - .. rst-class:: keepwithnext

       **Case 1:**

       .. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet06-gpimage09.png
          :scale: 70

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\dfrac{70}{\sin(28\degree)} = \dfrac{120}{\sin Y}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\angle Y \approx 53.6\degree`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\angle T = 180\degree - 28\degree - 53.6\degree
       \approx 98.4\degree`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\dfrac{t}{\sin(98.4\degree)} = \dfrac{70}{\sin(28\degree)}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`t \approx 147.5` km
     - .. rst-class:: keepwithnext

       **Case 2:**

       .. image:: ../images/mau5worksheet06-gpimage10.png
          :scale: 70

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\angle Y = 180\degree - 53.6\degree \approx 126.4\degree`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\angle T = 180\degree - 28\degree - 126.4\degree
       \approx 25.6\degree`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`\dfrac{t}{\sin(25.6\degree)} = \dfrac{70}{\sin(28\degree)}`

       .. rst-class:: solution

       :math:`t \approx 64.4` km

Answers
================================================================================

**1)** 26.45 cm or 10.26 cm

**2)** 10.0 m or 2.6 m

**3)** :math:`\angle C = 38.75\degree` and :math:`b = 4.7` cm;
:math:`\angle C = 141.25\degree` and :math:`b = 0.95` cm

**4)** :math:`\angle E = 56.4\degree` and :math:`f = 6.0` cm;
:math:`\angle E = 123.6\degree` and :math:`f = 2.7` cm

**5)** 147.5 km or 64.4 km
