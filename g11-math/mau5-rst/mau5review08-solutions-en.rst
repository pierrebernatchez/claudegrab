**8)** The arm of a crane is 20 m long. The angle of inclination of the
boom of the crane has a minimum value of 45° and a maximum value of
60°.

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**a)** Find an exact value for the vertical displacement of the boom of
the crane as it moves from its minimum to its maximum inclination
angles.

.. image:: ../images/mau5review08-gpimage07.png
   :align: center

.. image:: ../images/mau5review08-gpimage08.png
   :align: center

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\sin 45^\circ = \dfrac{y_1}{20} \Rightarrow \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} =
\dfrac{y_1}{20} \Rightarrow y_1 = \dfrac{20}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}}\right)
= \dfrac{20\sqrt{2}}{2} = 10\sqrt{2}`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\sin 60^\circ = \dfrac{y_2}{20} \Rightarrow \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} =
\dfrac{y_2}{20} \Rightarrow y_2 = \dfrac{20\sqrt{3}}{2} = 10\sqrt{3}`

.. rst-class:: solution

vertical displacement :math:`= y_2 - y_1 = 10\sqrt{3} - 10\sqrt{2} =
10(\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2})` m

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**b)** Find the value of this vertical displacement to the nearest
tenth of a metre.

.. rst-class:: solution

vertical displacement :math:`\approx 3.2` m

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**17)** A carousel, with a diameter of 20 m, has 12 horses spaced around
the circumference at equal distances. If the horses are numbered in
order, how far is it directly from the second horse to the fourth?

.. image:: ../images/mau5review08-gpimage20.png
   :align: center

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\dfrac{360^\circ}{12} = 30^\circ`, so the angle between adjacent
horses is 30°, and between horse 2 and horse 4 is :math:`2 \times
30^\circ = 60^\circ`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x^2 = 10^2 + 10^2 - 2(10)(10)\cos 60^\circ`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x = 10` m

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**18)** The angle between the shaft and the blade of a hockey stick is
120°. The shaft is 1.5 m long and the blade is 25 cm long. Determine
the straight line distance from the top of the shaft to the tip of the
blade.

.. image:: ../images/mau5review08-gpimage21.png
   :align: center

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x^2 = (1.5)^2 + (0.25)^2 - 2(1.5)(0.25)\cos 120^\circ`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x^2 = 2.6875`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`x \approx 1.64` m or 164 cm

.. rst-class:: keepwithnext

**19)** A support wire to the top of a newly planted tree is 15 m long.
It forms an angle of 30° to the ground. On the same side of the tree, a
second wire is also attached to the top of the tree, but it makes an
angle of 45° to the ground. Determine an exact expression for the
distance between the points where the two support wires are attached to
the ground.

.. image:: ../images/mau5review08-gpimage22.png
   :align: center

.. rst-class:: solution

Remember:

.. image:: ../images/mau5review08-gpimage02.png
   :align: center

.. image:: ../images/mau5review08-gpimage01.png
   :align: center

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\sin 30^\circ = \dfrac{z}{15} \Rightarrow \dfrac{1}{2} =
\dfrac{z}{15} \Rightarrow \dfrac{15}{2} = z`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\cos 30^\circ = \dfrac{y}{15} \Rightarrow \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} =
\dfrac{y}{15} \Rightarrow \dfrac{15\sqrt{3}}{2} = y`

.. rst-class:: solution

:math:`\tan 45^\circ = \dfrac{(15/2)}{x} \Rightarrow \dfrac{1}{1} =
\dfrac{(15/2)}{x} \Rightarrow x = \dfrac{15}{2}`

.. rst-class:: solution

distance between wires :math:`= y - x = \dfrac{15\sqrt{3}}{2} -
\dfrac{15}{2} = \dfrac{15(\sqrt{3}-1)}{2}` m, or :math:`7.5(\sqrt{3}-1)`
m
